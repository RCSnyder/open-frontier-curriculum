# Intellectual agency: learner architecture

Status: implementation authorized by the user on 2026-09-07.

## Constitution

The Open Frontier Curriculum develops independent entry into unfamiliar
questions, specialist study and responsible action. Minimum means a generative
foundation, not a short survey or a promise of universal expertise.

Four foundations organize the learner experience: Open Frontier (capability),
Human Worlds (context), Philosophy (judgment), and Expressive Command
(representation). Praxis integrates them. Development organizes entry and
progression; life contexts organize application; learning practices support all four.
AI is a tool, a subject of inquiry and a changed condition of agency, not its purpose.

The six legacy branches remain compatible source classifications, not six competing
learner-level foundations. Design is visible within representation, with technical
and practical connections. Mind and learning remains a visible supporting field.
Neither placement establishes a scholarly merger or removes existing material.

## Model and migration

Existing IDs remain immutable lookup keys. Foundation membership is an explicit
view, not an ID prefix migration. Subjects, child outcomes, sources, practice,
assessment and pathways retain distinct meanings. Philosophy problem families
remain subjects rather than being misrepresented as granular measurable outcomes.

The learner generator joins canonical records and reviewed exposition attachments.
It never manufactures prerequisite edges, reading assignments or evidence of mastery.
Known prerequisites and subsequent dependents come from the same graph. Unresolved
ordering is explicit. Source recommendations are not silently treated as assigned reading.

Existing routes remain accessible. Compatibility frontmatter and route notices
exclude duplicated legacy teaching from search while preserving its teaching body.
This intentionally changes file hashes; preservation is checked at the body level.
The new navigation has one subject home per
existing module or standalone philosophical family. Readable routes for the first
three complete journeys are explicit; remaining stable record routes do not change
when titles change. A future all-record slug migration requires aliases and tests.

## Build slices and acceptance

1. Model and generator: four foundation views, learning support, unique subject
   homes, typed prerequisite/source joins. Proof: focused unit and mutation tests.
2. Learner pages: consistent outcomes, readiness, readings, practice, assessment
   and onward links. Complete proof, commons and literary journeys reuse substantive
   teaching without presenting calendar weeks as universal requirements.
3. Navigation and visual design: usable home, foundation indexes, praxis,
   developmental routes, source discovery, project material separated from study.
   Proof: link checks, browser navigation, search, responsive screenshots.
4. Integration: regeneration checks run in existing audit and CI; document ownership
   and remaining intellectual limits. Proof: full local release gates.

## Non-goals and risks

No deletion of protected legacy teaching, fabricated new scholarly review, universal
age schedule, learner tracking service, automatic branch reclassification or ID reset.
An attractive graph cannot establish prerequisite truth. A consistent page cannot
turn an unreviewed source list into a complete course. Learner usability requires
human testing after the automated interaction checks.
