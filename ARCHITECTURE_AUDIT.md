# Architecture audit

## Learner architecture update: 2026-09-07

The [constitution](CONSTITUTION.md) and [RFC 0002](rfcs/curriculum/0002-intellectual-agency.md)
now distinguish the four learner-facing foundations from the six retained source
classifications. This is an organizational change, not evidence of academic branch
compression. Praxis, development, life contexts and learning support have separate roles.
The earlier source-architecture audit below remains relevant to scholarly coverage.

Decision: retain six trunks provisionally. Do not infer intellectual completeness
from counts, source links or a passing build. The implemented ownership policy is
[RFC 0001](rfcs/curriculum/0001-obelisk-reconciliation.md).

## Constitutional alignment

The telos remains lifelong formation and human authorship under abundant machine
intelligence. The eight verbs remain INHERIT, UNDERSTAND, JUDGE, CREATE, ACT,
RELATE, STEWARD and RENEW. Knowledge, life practice and formation are complementary
coordinates, not competing taxonomies. AI convenience does not determine what must
be internalized; responsibility, reconstruction and failure recognition remain human.

## Deletion and compression

TS23, TS24 and TS25 remain candidate labels, not admitted IDs. Their current
descriptions identify credible failures but do not establish optimal boundaries.
No one-shot author judgment can stand in for a completed compression experiment.

DR overlaps EX in rhetoric and composition, TS in constraint and systems design,
and ML in perception. Its strongest distinct claim is accountable spatial/material
representation under real use. The studio makes that claim testable. Retain DR
until evidence supports merging or splitting it; do not freeze it on aesthetic preference.

PH has source links but no explicit node prerequisite declarations. The audit
therefore marks these nodes scaffold rather than inventing a linear order among
philosophical traditions. This is a documentary gap, not a finding that the ideas
lack depth. Non-PH node records also lack explicit prerequisite placement.

## Interfaces and application

All 63 interfaces now have explicit endpoints. Four imported ML/DR labels were
corrected using actual canonical titles and retained as provenance. Every life
competency has a knowledge route and a pillar-level readiness, scope and referral
contract. Four routes explicitly remain partial. Routes do not replace supervised
practice, local law, clinical judgment or relational consent.

The seven detailed technical specializations, original technical module pages,
portfolio standards and Frontier 100 remain discoverable. Imported Frontier
summaries are routing material, not replacement technical evidence. Classes A-E
remain imported feasibility judgments, not independently revalidated forecasts.

## Global and temporal audit

The 72 civilizational anchors provide breadth of named encounters, not verified
editions, competent translation, community permission or adequate teaching.
The PH/EX routes require particular care over tradition, genre and transmission.
HW needs comparative institutional evidence beyond a US/Anglophone default.
ML requires population and setting limits; self-report AI studies cannot establish
causal deskilling. DR requires craft, architecture and visual traditions beyond
generic commercial interface practice.

Use primary works for their own questions and historical settings before treating
them as transferable examples. Pair global range with sustained whole-work reading,
specialist criticism and explicit translation choices. Name missing evidence rather
than adding prestige titles to satisfy a quota.

## Engineering and residual risk

Canonical structure, attached exposition, generated views and empirical evidence
have distinct ownership. Schema, reference, cycle, Frontier parity, navigation,
release and generation checks protect these boundaries. The manifest hashes
LF-normalized owned source/configuration, excluding itself, caches, site output,
reports and provenance snapshots. It is an integrity inventory, not a signed release.

Hash-checked review records do not establish independence, reviewer competence or
causal validity. Most source metadata remains unreviewed, no prospective learner
study was conducted, and whole-life efficacy is not established. See
[content maturity](CONTENT_MATURITY.md), [source maturity](SOURCE_MATURITY.md) and
[next research](NEXT_RESEARCH.md).
