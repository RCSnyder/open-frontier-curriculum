# Voice, Tone, Register & Persona

[Expressive Command](../foundations/representation.md)

## What you will develop

Produce recognizably different voices and registers without changing underlying facts; infer stance from linguistic evidence.

## Study and practice

This is a study outline. A complete practice sequence is not yet available.

- speaker
- implied author
- persona
- tone
- stance
- distance
- formality
- dialect
- sociolect
- code-switching
- irony
- reliability

### Describe voice and tone through observable lexical, syntactic, rhythmic, and rhetorical features. {#ex-lo-021}

Reconstruct

### Rewrite one factual content block in at least five registers while keeping core facts invariant. {#ex-lo-022}

Transfer

### Construct reliable and unreliable personae and show how evidence creates the reader's trust or suspicion. {#ex-lo-023}

Break / Bound

### Use irony and distance without making the intended stance unrecoverable. {#ex-lo-024}

Check / Defend

### Intended demonstration

Same-content multi-register composition + tone defense

This describes the intended standard, not a validated assessment or a claim of mastery.
