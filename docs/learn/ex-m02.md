# Diction, Semantics, Idiom & Lexical Precision

[Expressive Command](../foundations/representation.md)

## What you will develop

Choose words for exact meaning, implication, register, rhythm, and social effect; diagnose near-synonym differences.

## Before you begin

Recorded prerequisites:

- [Grammar & Sentence Architecture](ex-m01.md)

## Study and practice

This is a study outline. A complete practice sequence is not yet available.

- denotation
- connotation
- polysemy
- register
- collocation
- idiom
- metaphorical extension
- synonym contrast
- etymology as clue not proof

### Define a word operationally enough to separate it from close alternatives, with positive examples and boundary cases. {#ex-lo-005}

Reconstruct

### Choose among near-synonyms by denotation, connotation, register, collocation, rhythm, and audience. {#ex-lo-006}

Transfer

### Recognize idiomatic versus merely grammatical phrasing and revise unnatural collocations. {#ex-lo-007}

Break / Bound

### Use dictionaries/corpora as evidence while resisting etymological fallacies and false precision. {#ex-lo-008}

Check / Defend

### Intended demonstration

Definition contrasts + lexical substitution + boundary cases

This describes the intended standard, not a validated assessment or a claim of mastery.
