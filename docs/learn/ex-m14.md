# Integrated Expressive Command & Editorial Judgment

[Expressive Command](../foundations/representation.md)

## What you will develop

Express the same difficult content as explanation, argument, narrative, dialogue, lyric, speech, and concise summary; edit each toward exact intended effect.

## Study and practice

This is a study outline. A complete practice sequence is not yet available.

- intention
- audience
- medium
- form
- structure
- syntax
- diction
- image
- voice
- evidence
- rhythm
- revision
- deletion
- amplification
- ethical representation

### Choose form, register, structure, syntax, diction, imagery, and rhythm from intention rather than habit. {#ex-lo-053}

Reconstruct

### Express one difficult proposition successfully as analytic explanation, persuasive argument, narrative, scene, lyric, speech, and concise summary. {#ex-lo-054}

Transfer

### Edit at conceptual, structural, paragraph, sentence, and word levels in the correct order. {#ex-lo-055}

Break / Bound

### Defend expressive choices under changed audience/medium constraints and revise immediately when the premise changes. {#ex-lo-056}

Check / Defend

### Intended demonstration

Capstone portfolio + unseen transformation exam + final defense

This describes the intended standard, not a validated assessment or a claim of mastery.
