# Grammar & Sentence Architecture

[Expressive Command](../foundations/representation.md)

## What you will develop

Build, parse, combine, split, and deliberately vary English sentences while preserving exact logical relations.

## Before you begin

None

## Study and practice

This is a study outline. A complete practice sequence is not yet available.

- clause
- phrase
- subject/predicate
- coordination/subordination
- modification
- agreement
- tense/aspect
- punctuation
- parallelism
- marked word order

### Parse unfamiliar sentences into clauses and explain the function of major phrases and modifiers. {#ex-lo-001}

Reconstruct

### Generate at least five syntactically different sentences expressing the same logical proposition. {#ex-lo-002}

Transfer

### Use punctuation, coordination, subordination, and word order to control emphasis and relation rather than merely obey rules. {#ex-lo-003}

Break / Bound

### Diagnose ambiguity or unintended implication caused by grammatical structure and repair it without loss of nuance. {#ex-lo-004}

Check / Defend

### Intended demonstration

Parse + reconstruct + sentence transformation + defense

This describes the intended standard, not a validated assessment or a claim of mastery.
