# Dialogue, Drama & Subtext

[Expressive Command](../foundations/representation.md)

## What you will develop

Write speech that simultaneously performs action, reveals character, withholds information, and advances conflict.

## Study and practice

This is a study outline. A complete practice sequence is not yet available.

- speech act
- turn-taking
- subtext
- dramatic objective
- conflict
- interruption
- silence
- monologue
- soliloquy
- scene beat
- stage information

### Identify speech acts and objectives beneath dialogue lines rather than treating dialogue as information transfer only. {#ex-lo-029}

Reconstruct

### Write a two-person scene where each speaker pursues a different objective and important information remains implicit. {#ex-lo-030}

Transfer

### Use interruption, silence, rhythm, gesture/stage information, and turn length as meaning-bearing devices. {#ex-lo-031}

Break / Bound

### Transform explanatory prose into playable dramatic action without losing necessary content. {#ex-lo-032}

Check / Defend

### Intended demonstration

Subtext scene + speech-act analysis + performance defense

This describes the intended standard, not a validated assessment or a claim of mastery.
