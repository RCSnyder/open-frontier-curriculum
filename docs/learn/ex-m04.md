# Rhetoric, Argument, Audience & Occasion

[Expressive Command](../foundations/representation.md)

## What you will develop

Make a precise case for a skeptical audience, adapt it to occasion, and distinguish persuasion from proof.

## Before you begin

Recorded prerequisites:

- [Grammar & Sentence Architecture](ex-m01.md)

## Study and practice

This is a study outline. A complete practice sequence is not yet available.

- claim
- reason
- evidence
- warrant
- counterargument
- concession
- audience
- purpose
- exigence
- ethos/pathos/logos
- framing
- arrangement

### Map an argument into claim, reasons, evidence, warrants, assumptions, and counterarguments. {#ex-lo-013}

Reconstruct

### Adapt the same proposition to hostile, neutral, expert, and general audiences without changing what counts as evidence. {#ex-lo-014}

Transfer

### Distinguish rhetorical effectiveness from logical or empirical support. {#ex-lo-015}

Break / Bound

### Concede, qualify, rebut, or revise in response to a serious counterargument rather than caricaturing opposition. {#ex-lo-016}

Check / Defend

### Intended demonstration

Argument map + counterargument + audience transformation + oral defense

This describes the intended standard, not a validated assessment or a claim of mastery.
