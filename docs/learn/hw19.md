# Integrated Human Worlds Diagnosis & Institutional Design

[Human Worlds](../foundations/context.md)

## What you will develop

Produce and defend a global, historically situated diagnosis of a real institution/problem and design a reversible intervention with explicit failure tests.

## Study and practice

This is a study outline. A complete practice sequence is not yet available.

- multi-scale model
- competing explanations
- distribution
- legitimacy
- implementation
- second-order effects
- reversibility
- learning institutions

### Multi-scale actor / rule / power / feedback map {#hw19-n01}

Reconstruct the mechanism, apply it to an unseen case, produce a counterexample/boundary, identify evidence, and defend under changed assumptions.
