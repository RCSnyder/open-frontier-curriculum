# Imitation, Transformation & Register Translation

[Expressive Command](../foundations/representation.md)

## What you will develop

Preserve a chosen semantic core while radically altering voice, form, length, audience, or genre; imitate mechanisms without copying wording.

## Study and practice

This is a study outline. A complete practice sequence is not yet available.

- imitation
- pastiche
- paraphrase
- compression
- expansion
- recasting
- genre shift
- register shift
- constraint
- invariant meaning
- stylistic feature

### Identify a passage's generative style features without reducing style to surface adjectives. {#ex-lo-045}

Reconstruct

### Imitate structural mechanisms of a style using completely new subject matter and wording. {#ex-lo-046}

Transfer

### Paraphrase difficult prose into plain English, then restore complexity where simplification destroyed necessary distinctions. {#ex-lo-047}

Break / Bound

### Transform content across genre, length, voice, and register while explicitly tracking what must remain invariant. {#ex-lo-048}

Check / Defend

### Intended demonstration

Five-way transformation + style-feature defense

This describes the intended standard, not a validated assessment or a claim of mastery.
