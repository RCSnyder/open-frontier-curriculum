# Oral English, Cadence, Speech & Performance

[Expressive Command](../foundations/representation.md)

## What you will develop

Turn written English into intelligible, persuasive, memorable speech and compose with the ear as well as the eye.

## Study and practice

This is a study outline. A complete practice sequence is not yet available.

- breath group
- stress
- pause
- cadence
- emphasis
- pace
- pitch contour
- rhetorical period
- spoken register
- memorization
- delivery
- audience feedback

### Mark prose for breath, stress, pause, pace, and emphasis, then revise syntax to improve live intelligibility. {#ex-lo-049}

Reconstruct

### Deliver a short argument extemporaneously from an outline rather than reading sentences. {#ex-lo-050}

Transfer

### Use rhetorical repetition, parallelism, cadence, and timing without sounding accidental or inflated. {#ex-lo-051}

Break / Bound

### Diagnose the difference between a text that reads well silently and one that works when heard once. {#ex-lo-052}

Check / Defend

### Intended demonstration

Read-aloud revision + extemporaneous speech + oral defense

This describes the intended standard, not a validated assessment or a claim of mastery.
