# Cohesion, Paragraphs & Information Flow

[Expressive Command](../foundations/representation.md)

## What you will develop

Control what the reader knows, expects, and notices from sentence to sentence and paragraph to paragraph.

## Before you begin

Recorded prerequisites:

- [Grammar & Sentence Architecture](ex-m01.md)
- [Diction, Semantics, Idiom & Lexical Precision](ex-m02.md)

## Study and practice

This is a study outline. A complete practice sequence is not yet available.

- topic/focus
- old-to-new information
- given/new
- reference
- transition
- paragraph unity
- progression
- emphasis
- periodic/cumulative movement

### Arrange sentences so given information leads readers toward new information with minimal processing confusion. {#ex-lo-009}

Reconstruct

### Build paragraphs with a discernible controlling movement rather than a pile of related statements. {#ex-lo-010}

Transfer

### Control pronoun/reference chains, transitions, parallel structure, and emphasis across multiple paragraphs. {#ex-lo-011}

Break / Bound

### Rewrite a confusing passage by changing order and information structure without simplifying the underlying idea. {#ex-lo-012}

Check / Defend

### Intended demonstration

Reorder/revise passage + unseen exposition problem

This describes the intended standard, not a validated assessment or a claim of mastery.
