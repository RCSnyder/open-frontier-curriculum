# Social Evidence, Interpretation & Comparative Method

[Human Worlds](../foundations/context.md)

## What you will develop

Separate observation, inference, model, interpretation, and normative claim; build competing causal stories and identify discriminating evidence.

## Before you begin

None

## Study and practice

This is a study outline. A complete practice sequence is not yet available.

- claim ledger
- source criticism
- construct validity
- case comparison
- process tracing
- qualitative/quantitative triangulation
