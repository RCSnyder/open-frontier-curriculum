# Poetry: Rhythm, Meter, Line & Sound

[Expressive Command](../foundations/representation.md)

## What you will develop

Hear, scan, and deliberately organize sound and line so form contributes to meaning rather than decorating it.

## Study and practice

This is a study outline. A complete practice sequence is not yet available.

- stress
- rhythm
- foot
- meter
- iamb
- cadence
- rhyme
- alliteration
- assonance
- consonance
- line
- enjambment
- caesura
- stanza
- free verse

### Scan common accentual-syllabic lines and distinguish meter from actual performed rhythm. {#ex-lo-033}

Reconstruct

### Use rhyme, stress, alliteration, assonance, consonance, caesura, and repetition deliberately. {#ex-lo-034}

Transfer

### Explain how line breaks and enjambment alter syntax, pace, emphasis, and ambiguity. {#ex-lo-035}

Break / Bound

### Compose one idea in metrical verse, free verse, and rhythmic prose, then defend the formal tradeoffs. {#ex-lo-036}

Check / Defend

### Intended demonstration

Scansion + line-break transformation + compose under form constraint

This describes the intended standard, not a validated assessment or a claim of mastery.
