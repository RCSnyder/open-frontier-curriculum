# Whole-work interpretation studio

Canonical target: **EX-M11**; interfaces with **EX-M07**, **EX-M08**, **EX-M09**,
**EX-M12**, **EX-M13**, **PH-M03** and **HW14**.

## Encounter before commentary

Read a complete play rather than a plot summary. The existing Hamlet route
(`SRC-EX-014`, `SRC-EX-022`) points to the
[Folger reading edition](https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-works/hamlet/read/).
Use a legally available edition and record its editor; do not reproduce the edition
in this repository. A first reading may use glossary support but not AI summaries
as a substitute for the work. Accommodations can change medium without removing
the encounter with the complete dramatic structure.

Keep an act-by-act record of what each character knows, asserts, conceals and
misunderstands. Separate what an audience sees from what a character can know.
Record two passages whose significance changes after later scenes. Do not resolve
every contradiction into a single psychological diagnosis.

## Form and interpretation

Choose a short scene and mark turn-taking, interruptions, changes of register,
verse/prose shifts, imagery and explicit stage directions. Distinguish textual
evidence from a director's staging choice. Read the scene aloud in two plausible
ways; explain how cadence or silence changes an inference without changing words.

Construct two competing readings of an important action. Each reading must explain
awkward evidence rather than ignoring it. Identify where genre, dramatic convention,
historical vocabulary or textual variation constrains interpretation. An attractive
theme is not evidence that every image supports it.

## Transformation and comparison

Transform the scene into a short prose narrative with a restricted point of view.
Then reconstruct it as dialogue. Explain what is lost: timing, ambiguity, shared
attention, dramatic irony or the audience's interpretive labor. This makes form an
object of inquiry rather than packaging for a message.

Pair the work with a complete short work from another dramatic or narrative
tradition, chosen with qualified source/translation guidance. Reconstruct each
work on its own terms before comparison. Do not make the second work an exotic
illustration of categories fixed by the first. Record translation choices and
avoid treating a national label as a homogeneous tradition.

## Unseen assessment

An examiner supplies a short unfamiliar passage and enough context to avoid a
trivia test. Produce a close reading with an alternative interpretation, then a
brief performance or transformation. Explain one point where more context could
reverse the reading. After a delay, reconnect a previously read scene to the whole
work without a generated synopsis.

Internalize enough vocabulary and sequence to reason fluently. Reconstruct
interpretations and evaluate evidence. Retrieve edition history and unfamiliar
terms. AI can supply an adversarial reading only after the learner's own attempt;
the learner must find its unsupported inference. No generated essay establishes
independent literary judgment.

## Examiner guidance

Assess attention to language, form, whole-work relations, contextual restraint,
alternative readings and revision. Do not reward quotation quantity, fashionable
terminology or agreement with an examiner's preferred interpretation. Strong work
can defend ambiguity precisely. Plot recall alone is insufficient; imaginative
novelty without textual warrant is also insufficient.

Use [examiner calibration](examiner-calibration.md). This studio develops one
implementation route, not proof that the branch's entire canon is globally mature.
