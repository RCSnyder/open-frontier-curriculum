# Home and practical independence

[Life in practice](index.md)

## Readiness and care

Demonstrated tool and hygiene safety before supervised practical work

No live mains, gas, structural modification, hazardous chemical mixing or work at height. Use manuals and local safety rules.

Licensed electrician, gas technician, structural engineer, poison service or emergency services as appropriate

Stop at uncertainty about energy isolation, structural support, food safety or hazardous substances. Use benign simulations where necessary.
