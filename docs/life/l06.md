# Work and craft

[Life in practice](index.md)

## Readiness and care

Supervised apprenticeship before independent accountable work

Respect labor protections, accessibility, authorization and workplace safety. Do not use unpaid risky work as an assessment shortcut.

Qualified supervisor, safety officer, occupational specialist or relevant professional

External standards and user acceptance matter; confidential work may be assessed through redacted evidence.

## Craft apprenticeship & standards {#l06-c01}

**Understand:** skill decomposition; standards; tacit knowledge; deliberate practice; quality

**Judge:** Know what 'good work' looks like and how to learn from expert critique

**Practice:** Pursue one craft/technical skill for 100+ deliberate hours

**Evidence:** Before/after portfolio + external review
