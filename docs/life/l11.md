# Culture and meaning

[Life in practice](index.md)

## Readiness and care

Supported whole-work encounter before advanced comparative interpretation

Respect community protocols, sacred/restricted knowledge, attribution and copyright. Do not infer absence from an incomplete archive.

Librarian, translator, curator, tradition-bearer or appropriately authorized community expert

Distinguish historical influence, interpretation, aesthetic judgment and personal preference; retain rival readings.

## World-historical orientation {#l11-c01}

**Understand:** major eras; states/empires; religions; trade; industrialization; colonialism; decolonization; globalization

**Judge:** Use periodization as a tool, not destiny; avoid presentism

**Practice:** Build a comparative world timeline

**Evidence:** Timeline + causal themes

## Religions, philosophies & worldviews {#l11-c02}

**Understand:** major religious/philosophical traditions; ritual; metaphysics; ethics; community

**Judge:** Describe traditions in terms adherents could recognize while retaining critical inquiry

**Practice:** Compare primary texts/practices from several traditions

## Literature & human experience {#l11-c03}

**Understand:** epic; drama; poetry; novel; essay; oral traditions; global literatures

**Judge:** Read for form, historical voice, moral imagination, and ambiguity rather than plot summary

**Practice:** Sustained close reading across eras/regions

**Evidence:** Reading portfolio + seminar
