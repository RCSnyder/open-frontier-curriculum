# Nature, place and stewardship

[Life in practice](index.md)

## Readiness and care

Field safety and local orientation before independent measurement or intervention

Do not enter hazardous sites, disturb protected habitats or perform engineering interventions without permission and expertise.

Environmental professional, engineer, land manager, weather authority or emergency services

Use real observations with uncertainty and seasonality; stop when weather, access or competence limits are exceeded.

## Earth systems & ecology {#l12-c01}

**Understand:** climate; water; soils; biogeochemical cycles; ecosystems; biodiversity; feedbacks

**Judge:** Distinguish local observation, model projection, uncertainty, and value choices

**Practice:** Field/system map of a local ecosystem/resource

**Evidence:** Map + measurements + source review
