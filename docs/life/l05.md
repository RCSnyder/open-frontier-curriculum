# Money and financial decisions

[Life in practice](index.md)

## Readiness and care

Fractions, percentages, probability and contract-reading practice

Education is not personalized financial, tax or legal advice. No real-money speculation is required.

Qualified accountant, regulated fiduciary/adviser, lawyer or consumer-protection service; check credentials and conflicts

Synthetic budgets and contracts; disclose fees, assumptions, jurisdiction and downside scenarios.

## Cash flow, budgeting & tradeoffs {#l05-c01}

**Understand:** income; expenses; fixed/variable costs; cash flow; inflation; opportunity cost

**Judge:** Build budgets that reflect values and uncertainty rather than false precision

**Practice:** Maintain a real/simulated monthly budget

**Evidence:** Reconciled cash-flow model
