# Self and commitments

[Life in practice](index.md)

## Readiness and care

Grounding with trusted support; independent planning after basic literacy and numeracy

Reflection is not diagnosis. Do not require disclosure of trauma or private values for assessment.

A qualified mental-health professional for persistent distress or impairment; local emergency services for immediate danger

Use redacted or fictional decision records; assess process rather than compliance with an examiner's values.

## Values, commitments & goal hierarchy {#l01-c01}

**Understand:** values; goals; identity; opportunity cost; roles

**Judge:** Distinguish chosen commitments from inherited/status incentives; resolve conflicts among goals

**Practice:** Write and revise a one-year / five-year goal hierarchy

**Evidence:** Goal map + decision postmortem
