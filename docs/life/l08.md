# Learning and research

[Life in practice](index.md)

## Readiness and care

Source literacy before independent inquiry; quantitative prerequisites before causal analysis

No human-subject research without appropriate ethics review and consent. Public data can still create privacy risks.

Librarian, domain researcher, statistician or ethics review body

Record sources, exclusions, uncertainty and reproducibility; do not reward unsupported certainty.

**Judge:** Use authority contextually; trace claims upstream

**Practice:** Build a source map for a contested question
