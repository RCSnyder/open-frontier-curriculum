# Communication

[Life in practice](index.md)

## Readiness and care

Choose texts and audiences appropriate to language fluency; offer accessible media

Do not confuse persuasive effectiveness with truth or legitimacy. Obtain consent for recordings and protect private speech.

Qualified interpreter for high-stakes medical or legal communication

Assess accuracy, listening and audience fit separately from accent, status or rhetorical polish.

## Close reading & interpretation {#l07-c01}

**Understand:** genre; context; inference; ambiguity; rhetoric; evidence

**Judge:** Distinguish what a text says from what the reader projects

**Practice:** Annotate and defend an interpretation of a difficult text

**Evidence:** Close-reading oral defense

## Expository writing {#l07-c02}

**Understand:** thesis; structure; paragraph logic; evidence; revision; style

**Judge:** Prefer clarity and warranted precision over impressive vagueness

**Practice:** Write and substantially revise a researched essay

**Evidence:** Draft history + final + defense

## Argument & rhetoric {#l07-c03}

**Understand:** claims; reasons; warrants; audience; ethos/pathos/logos; framing; fallacies

**Judge:** Persuade without hiding decisive evidence or exploiting vulnerability

**Practice:** Deliver opposing arguments and strongest rebuttal

**Evidence:** Speech/debate + self-critique

**Practice:** Conduct a long-form interview and produce fair synthesis

## Multilingual / cross-cultural communication {#l07-c06}

**Understand:** language learning; translation; register; code-switching; pragmatics; cultural conventions

**Judge:** Treat translation as interpretation and avoid assuming one's norms are universal

**Practice:** Reach functional competence in another language where feasible; compare translations

**Evidence:** Conversation + translation commentary
