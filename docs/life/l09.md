# Technology and AI

[Life in practice](index.md)

## Readiness and care

Benign local computing before delegated agents or networked experiments

Use owned or explicitly authorized systems. No secrets in prompts; least privilege, spending limits, monitoring and rollback before delegation.

Security professional, system owner or data-protection officer for sensitive systems

Separate unaided explanation from AI-produced output; verify recovery without the model.
