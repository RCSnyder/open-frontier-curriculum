# Civic and legal life

[Life in practice](index.md)

## Readiness and care

Local institutional literacy and primary-document reading before public action

Law varies by jurisdiction and date. Curriculum interpretation is not legal advice or a guarantee of rights enforcement.

Qualified local lawyer, legal-aid service, ombudsman or relevant public office

Use current official rules, appeal routes and lawful participation; never require partisan agreement.

## Civics, law & rights {#l10-c01}

**Understand:** state; constitution; rights; courts; regulation; local government; legal process

**Judge:** Know which rule system applies, what rights/obligations exist, and where recourse lies

**Practice:** Trace a real issue through institutions

**Evidence:** Authority/recourse map

**Understand:** elections; parties; coalitions; public opinion; media systems; propaganda; agenda setting

**Practice:** Analyze a live/past campaign or information event
