# Relationships and family

[Life in practice](index.md)

## Readiness and care

Supported dialogue before independent mediation; consent throughout

No compelled intimacy, disclosure or reconciliation. Do not mediate abuse as an ordinary symmetric disagreement.

Safeguarding lead, qualified counselor, domestic-abuse service or emergency services when safety is threatened

Role-play or consented observation; protect identities and permit withdrawal.

## Listening & perspective taking {#l03-c01}

**Understand:** active listening; empathy; inference vs observation; cultural context

**Judge:** Represent another person's position fairly before responding

**Practice:** Structured listening interview

**Evidence:** Partner-rated summary accuracy

## Friendship & trust {#l03-c02}

**Understand:** reciprocity; reliability; disclosure; boundaries; repair

**Judge:** Distinguish intimacy from dependency, loyalty from enabling, trust from naivety

**Practice:** Maintain/repair a real commitment

**Evidence:** Reflection + counterpart feedback

## Consent, boundaries & autonomy {#l03-c03}

**Understand:** consent; coercion; privacy; power; refusal; negotiation

**Judge:** Recognize power asymmetry and preserve the other's meaningful ability to say no

**Practice:** Role-play boundary/consent negotiations

**Evidence:** Observed scenario + redo

## Family systems & caregiving {#l03-c05}

**Understand:** family roles; development; dependency; care work; kinship; intergenerational obligations

**Judge:** Balance autonomy, duty, limited resources, and diverse family forms without one idealized template

**Practice:** Complete a real care/service responsibility

**Evidence:** Care log + reflection + recipient feedback where appropriate
