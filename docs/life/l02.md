# Health and care

[Life in practice](index.md)

## Readiness and care

Supported care navigation; age-appropriate consent and safeguarding

No self-diagnosis, medication changes or unsupervised clinical intervention. Exercise and nutrition tasks must accommodate disability and medical restrictions.

Physician, pharmacist, registered dietitian or qualified mental-health professional as relevant; urgent services for emergencies

Use a simulated consultation, questions and source appraisal; never grade private health outcomes.

## Health information & care navigation {#l02-c01}

**Understand:** health literacy; evidence; screening; prevention; medical system; informed consent

**Judge:** Know when self-care is appropriate and when qualified professional help is needed

**Practice:** Compare sources/services and prepare for a clinical visit

**Evidence:** Health-source audit + care-navigation scenario
