# Learner stages

Readiness is demonstrated, not assigned by age. These are entry routes, not complete stage-specific curricula.

## Grounding

**Readiness:** Supported reading, observation, counting and consent; accessible alternatives agreed with a facilitator

**Practice:** Read a complete age-appropriate work; keep observations; perform a safe household task with support

**Assessment:** Explain and demonstrate without generated answers; identify when help is needed

AI permissions: A0, A1, A5

Entry routes: [L01-C02](life-coverage.md#l01-c02), [L03-C01](life-coverage.md#l03-c01), [L04-C01](life-coverage.md#l04-c01), [L07-C01](life-coverage.md#l07-c01)

## Foundations

**Readiness:** Algebra, sustained reading and source comparison; diagnose gaps before advanced texts

**Practice:** Reconstruct a proof, parse a paragraph, compare evidence and measure retained learning

**Assessment:** Closed-resource reconstruction and an unfamiliar example

AI permissions: A0, A1, A2, A5

Entry routes: [TS-F01](curriculum-index.md#ts-f01), [EX-M01](curriculum-index.md#ex-m01), [HW01](curriculum-index.md#hw01), [ML01](curriculum-index.md#ml01)

## Apprenticeship

**Readiness:** Relevant foundations, safety induction and a qualified supervisor

**Practice:** Complete and revise a real task under external quality standards

**Assessment:** Observed execution, correction of failure and supervisor critique

AI permissions: A0, A1, A2, A4, A5

Entry routes: [ML06](curriculum-index.md#ml06), [TS-F21](curriculum-index.md#ts-f21), [DR07](curriculum-index.md#dr07), [L06-C01](life-coverage.md#l06-c01)

## Independent formation

**Readiness:** Reliable self-assessment, prerequisite closure and practical risk judgment

**Practice:** Design a personal curriculum, conduct field inquiry and maintain an accountable project

**Assessment:** Independent review, unfamiliar transfer and delayed retest

AI permissions: A0, A1, A2, A3, A4, A5

Entry routes: [ML16](curriculum-index.md#ml16), [HW19](curriculum-index.md#hw19), [EX-M14](curriculum-index.md#ex-m14), [DR13](curriculum-index.md#dr13)

## Advanced Open Frontier

**Readiness:** Relevant technical core plus serious specialization depth and domain safety review

**Practice:** Reproduce, extend and integrate a specialization result; publish limits and negative findings

**Assessment:** Expert-reviewed artifact, novel perturbation, provenance audit and human defense

AI permissions: A0, A1, A2, A3, A4, A5

Entry routes: [TS-F11](curriculum-index.md#ts-f11), [TS-F22](curriculum-index.md#ts-f22), [ML15](curriculum-index.md#ml15), [HW06](curriculum-index.md#hw06)

## Lifelong renewal

**Readiness:** Prior learning evidence and an honest account of changed capacities or circumstances

**Practice:** Revisit a former capability, learn an unfamiliar domain and revise commitments

**Assessment:** Delayed retrieval, new-context performance, responsible help-seeking and decision review

AI permissions: A0, A1, A2, A3, A4, A5

Entry routes: [ML13](curriculum-index.md#ml13), [ML16](curriculum-index.md#ml16), [L12-C06](life-coverage.md#l12-c06)
