# Life competency routes

Generated structural view. Curated exposition and evidence remain separately identified.

Disciplinary routes are prerequisites, not substitutes for domain practice or professional advice.

## L01-C01: Values, commitments & goal hierarchy {#l01-c01}

Knowledge routes: [ML08](curriculum-index.md#ml08), [PH-S18](curriculum-index.md#ph-s18)

**Knowledge to study:** values; goals; identity; opportunity cost; roles

**Judgment to form:** Distinguish chosen commitments from inherited/status incentives; resolve conflicts among goals

**Practice:** Write and revise a one-year / five-year goal hierarchy

**Evidence:** Goal map + decision postmortem

**Readiness:** Grounding with trusted support; independent planning after basic literacy and numeracy

**Scope:** Reflection is not diagnosis. Do not require disclosure of trauma or private values for assessment.

**Referral:** A qualified mental-health professional for persistent distress or impairment; local emergency services for immediate danger

**Evidence boundary:** Use redacted or fictional decision records; assess process rather than compliance with an examiner's values.
