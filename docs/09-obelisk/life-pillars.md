# Life pillars

Branches organize knowledge. Life pillars organize competent action. A life pillar may draw from several trunks.

## L01 | Self, Character & Agency

Direct one's attention, commitments, habits, decisions, identity, and use of time.

**Primary trunks:** ML/PH

**Evidence:** Decision journal; 12-week self-governance project

### Competencies

- `L01-C01` **Values, commitments & goal hierarchy**: Write and revise a one-year / five-year goal hierarchy Evidence: Goal map + decision postmortem
- `L01-C02` **Attention & time governance**: Run a 3-week attention/time experiment Evidence: Time audit + before/after evidence
- `L01-C03` **Habits & environment design**: Build one useful habit and remove one harmful loop Evidence: Habit protocol + adherence data
- `L01-C04` **Emotion & self-regulation**: Use a regulation protocol in a real stressful decision Evidence: Reflection + observed/recorded outcome
- `L01-C05` **Decision process & calibration**: Maintain decision/forecast journal Evidence: Calibration and decision review
- `L01-C06` **Meaning, identity & mortality**: Write and defend a personal philosophy provisionally Evidence: Essay + dialogue + revision

## L02 | Body, Health & Vitality

Understand and care for the human body across prevention, movement, nutrition, sleep, mental health, sexuality, illness, and care navigation.

**Primary trunks:** TS/ML/HW

**Evidence:** Health literacy audit + sustained practice + emergency scenario

### Competencies

- `L02-C01` **Health information & care navigation**: Compare sources/services and prepare for a clinical visit Evidence: Health-source audit + care-navigation scenario
- `L02-C02` **Sleep, recovery & circadian health**: Run a sleep/recovery experiment Evidence: Diary + performance comparison
- `L02-C03` **Nutrition & food environment**: Plan/cook a week of nutritious food within constraints Evidence: Meal plan + cost/nutrition audit + cooking demo
- `L02-C04` **Movement, strength & aerobic capacity**: Complete a progressive training block Evidence: Baseline/post measures + technique check
- `L02-C05` **Mental health & help-seeking**: Build support/resources plan; practice supportive conversation Evidence: Scenario + resource map
- `L02-C06` **Sexual/reproductive health, consent & safety**: Analyze scenarios and locate valid services/information Evidence: Scenario defense + information audit

## L03 | Relationships, Family & Care

Build trustworthy relationships; communicate needs; establish consent/boundaries; repair conflict; nurture family/community; care for dependents.

**Primary trunks:** HW/EX/ML/PH

**Evidence:** Observed dialogue + care/service evidence

### Competencies

- `L03-C01` **Listening & perspective taking**: Structured listening interview Evidence: Partner-rated summary accuracy
- `L03-C02` **Friendship & trust**: Maintain/repair a real commitment Evidence: Reflection + counterpart feedback
- `L03-C03` **Consent, boundaries & autonomy**: Role-play boundary/consent negotiations Evidence: Observed scenario + redo
- `L03-C04` **Conflict, negotiation & repair**: Mediate or resolve a structured conflict Evidence: Agreement/repair record + postmortem
- `L03-C05` **Family systems & caregiving**: Complete a real care/service responsibility Evidence: Care log + reflection + recipient feedback where appropriate
- `L03-C06` **Belonging, hospitality & community**: Host/organize a recurring small community activity Evidence: Participation + learning review

## L04 | Home, Food & Practical Independence

Operate a household: food, cleaning, maintenance, tools, transport, logistics, safety, emergency readiness, and basic repair.

**Primary trunks:** TS/ML/DR

**Evidence:** Live practical demonstrations

### Competencies

- `L04-C01` **Food preparation & kitchen systems**: Cook multiple complete meals from raw ingredients Evidence: Live cooking + food-safety check
- `L04-C02` **Cleaning, laundry & household hygiene**: Run a household maintenance schedule Evidence: Inspection + materials reasoning
- `L04-C03` **Tools, fasteners & basic repair**: Repair or assemble several real objects Evidence: Live tool demonstration + repair artifact
- `L04-C04` **Utilities, home systems & energy**: Map a home's major systems and shutoffs Evidence: System map + emergency drill
- `L04-C05` **Transport, navigation & logistics**: Plan and execute a multi-leg real journey Evidence: Route plan + contingency + execution
- `L04-C06` **Emergency readiness & first response**: Complete recognized first-aid/CPR training where available and run drills Evidence: Credential/equivalent + scenario

## L05 | Money, Property & Financial Agency

Manage cash flow, savings, debt, credit, insurance, investing, taxes, contracts, fraud, and long-horizon financial choices.

**Primary trunks:** HW/TS/ML

**Evidence:** Personal finance model + product comparison + scam audit

### Competencies

- `L05-C01` **Cash flow, budgeting & tradeoffs**: Maintain a real/simulated monthly budget Evidence: Reconciled cash-flow model
- `L05-C02` **Saving, compounding & liquidity**: Model savings paths under shocks Evidence: Scenario model + decision memo
- `L05-C03` **Credit, debt & borrowing**: Compare three borrowing offers Evidence: Cost table + contract red-flag audit
- `L05-C04` **Risk, insurance & protection**: Analyze auto/home/health/disability/life examples appropriate to stage Evidence: Coverage-gap memo
- `L05-C05` **Investing, diversification & retirement**: Build a simple policy statement and stress test Evidence: Investment policy + drawdown scenario
- `L05-C06` **Taxes, contracts, fraud & consumer defense**: Complete a sample return/contract/scam investigation Evidence: Document audit + recourse plan

## L06 | Work, Craft, Enterprise & Service

Develop valuable skill, manage projects, collaborate, operate systems, understand organizations/enterprise, and serve real users or communities.

**Primary trunks:** TS/HW/EX/ML/DR

**Evidence:** Long real-world project + external stakeholder

### Competencies

- `L06-C01` **Craft apprenticeship & standards**: Pursue one craft/technical skill for 100+ deliberate hours Evidence: Before/after portfolio + external review
- `L06-C02` **Project formulation & execution**: Run a 12–24 week project Evidence: Project log + delivered artifact
- `L06-C03` **Teamwork, leadership & followership**: Rotate roles on a real team Evidence: 360 feedback + incident review
- `L06-C04` **Operations, quality & reliability**: Map and improve a recurring process Evidence: Before/after process metrics
- `L06-C05` **Enterprise, customers & value creation**: Serve a real customer/user or run micro-enterprise simulation Evidence: User evidence + unit economics
- `L06-C06` **Career, reputation & service**: Build portfolio/CV, conduct informational interviews, contribute publicly Evidence: External review + opportunity decision memo

## L07 | Communication, Language & Public Expression

Read, write, speak, listen, persuade, narrate, explain, debate, and communicate across cultures and media.

**Primary trunks:** EX/PH/HW/DR

**Evidence:** Writing/speech portfolio + live defense

### Competencies

- `L07-C01` **Close reading & interpretation**: Annotate and defend an interpretation of a difficult text Evidence: Close-reading oral defense
- `L07-C02` **Expository writing**: Write and substantially revise a researched essay Evidence: Draft history + final + defense
- `L07-C03` **Argument & rhetoric**: Deliver opposing arguments and strongest rebuttal Evidence: Speech/debate + self-critique
- `L07-C04` **Narrative & imagination**: Write/perform a narrative artifact Evidence: Workshop + revision
- `L07-C05` **Listening, conversation & interviewing**: Conduct a long-form interview and produce fair synthesis Evidence: Interview record + participant check
- `L07-C06` **Multilingual / cross-cultural communication**: Reach functional competence in another language where feasible; compare translations Evidence: Conversation + translation commentary

## L08 | Knowledge, Learning & Research

Search, evaluate sources, build knowledge, study effectively, reason statistically/causally, synthesize fields, and teach.

**Primary trunks:** ML/PH/TS/EX

**Evidence:** Independent research + unfamiliar-domain challenge

### Competencies

- `L08-C01` **Information search & source ecology**: Build a source map for a contested question Evidence: Search log + source genealogy
- `L08-C02` **Learning system design**: Design a 6-week study program Evidence: Delayed no-AI retest
- `L08-C03` **Quantitative and causal reasoning**: Audit a public quantitative claim Evidence: Reanalysis + causal critique
- `L08-C04` **Research design & inquiry**: Design and execute a small research project Evidence: Preregistered/declared plan + report
- `L08-C05` **Synthesis & knowledge architecture**: Create a teachable synthesis across 10+ sources Evidence: Synthesis map + adversarial review
- `L08-C06` **Teaching & mentorship**: Teach a novice and measure learning Evidence: Pre/post evidence + lesson revision

## L09 | Technology, Computing & AI

Understand computational systems, digital infrastructure, privacy/security, AI capabilities/limits, delegation, evaluation, and governance.

**Primary trunks:** TS/PH/HW/ML

**Evidence:** Build + threat model + agent eval + fallback

### Competencies

- `L09-C01` **Computational thinking & algorithms**: Implement and compare simple algorithms Evidence: Code + complexity/assumption defense
- `L09-C02` **Software, data & network literacy**: Build a small networked system and break it Evidence: System diagram + incident exercise
- `L09-C03` **Cybersecurity, privacy & identity**: Harden personal digital setup Evidence: Threat model + recovery drill
- `L09-C04` **AI mental models & capability limits**: Compare models on a controlled task suite Evidence: Eval report + limitation statement
- `L09-C05` **AI delegation, provenance & verification**: Run an agentic project with hidden tests Evidence: Audit log + human defense
- `L09-C06` **Digital governance, rights & concentration**: Analyze a platform/model governance case Evidence: Governance map + reform options

## L10 | Civic, Legal & Institutional Agency

Understand states, law, rights, public finance, media, organizations, collective action, power, and participation.

**Primary trunks:** HW/PH/EX

**Evidence:** Institution trace + civic action + public decision

### Competencies

- `L10-C01` **Civics, law & rights**: Trace a real issue through institutions Evidence: Authority/recourse map
- `L10-C02` **Public finance & collective provision**: Analyze a public budget/policy Evidence: Incidence + tradeoff memo
- `L10-C03` **Political competition & media**: Analyze a live/past campaign or information event Evidence: Source/media map + causal account
- `L10-C04` **Organizations, power & institutional behavior**: Map a school/company/public agency Evidence: Power/incentive map + redesign
- `L10-C05` **Deliberation, negotiation & pluralism**: Run a structured public deliberation Evidence: Decision record + dissent statement
- `L10-C06` **Civic participation & service**: Complete a local civic/service project Evidence: Public artifact + impact/postmortem

## L11 | Culture, History, Beauty & Meaning

Encounter world history, literature, arts, music, architecture, religions/worldviews, rituals, philosophy, and the search for meaning.

**Primary trunks:** EX/DR/HW/PH

**Evidence:** Comparative seminar + criticism + original work

### Competencies

- `L11-C01` **World-historical orientation**: Build a comparative world timeline Evidence: Timeline + causal themes
- `L11-C02` **Religions, philosophies & worldviews**: Compare primary texts/practices from several traditions Evidence: Comparative seminar + reflection
- `L11-C03` **Literature & human experience**: Sustained close reading across eras/regions Evidence: Reading portfolio + seminar
- `L11-C04` **Visual art, architecture, music & performance**: Analyze works in person when possible Evidence: Field notes + comparative criticism
- `L11-C05` **Taste, canon, criticism & curation**: Curate a small exhibition/anthology/program Evidence: Curatorial statement + blind ranking
- `L11-C06` **Meaning, ritual, memory & cultural creation**: Create/participate in a meaningful cultural artifact or oral-history project Evidence: Artifact + context/provenance defense

## L12 | Nature, Place, Risk & Future Stewardship

Understand Earth systems, ecology, energy, food/water/material flows, cities/place, hazards, resilience, long-term futures, and intergenerational responsibility.

**Primary trunks:** TS/HW/PH/DR

**Evidence:** Field systems map + resilience / future decision dossier

### Competencies

- `L12-C01` **Earth systems & ecology**: Field/system map of a local ecosystem/resource Evidence: Map + measurements + source review
- `L12-C02` **Energy, materials & industrial metabolism**: Trace an everyday product/energy service from resource to waste Evidence: Lifecycle/energy flow model
- `L12-C03` **Food, agriculture & water systems**: Trace a food/water system Evidence: System map + intervention critique
- `L12-C04` **Place, cities & built environment**: Conduct a neighborhood/place audit Evidence: Map + observation + redesign
- `L12-C05` **Hazards, resilience & continuity**: Build household/team continuity plan and run a drill Evidence: Scenario performance + after-action report
- `L12-C06` **Futures & intergenerational stewardship**: Write a 25-year decision under multiple futures Evidence: Scenario tree + reversible strategy + defense
