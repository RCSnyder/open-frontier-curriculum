# Design & Representation

Perception, form, diagrams, visualization, typography, spatial design, objects, interfaces, multimodal composition, aesthetics, prototyping, critique and curation.

**Imported editorial label:** `candidate-compression-test`. This is not a current evidence grade.
See [publication status and evidence](../../project/evidence.md).

## Deletion test

Visual-spatial, interface and aesthetic-functional failures recur even when technical and verbal competence remain intact.

| `DR11` | Prototyping, User Research, Critique & Iteration | Turn uncertain assumptions into prototypes and observations; revise without overfitting to one user. |
