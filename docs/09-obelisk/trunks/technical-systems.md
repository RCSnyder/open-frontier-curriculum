# Technical Systems

Mathematics, inference, computation, physics, chemistry, biology, engineering, manufacturing, measurement, operations, safety, reliability and security.

**Imported editorial label:** `live-foundation`. This is not a current evidence grade.
See [publication status and evidence](../../project/evidence.md).

## Deletion test

The learner cannot independently model, measure, build, operate or audit physical and technical systems.

## Modules

| ID | Module | Exit capability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `TS-F01` | Mathematical reasoning + proof | Own definitions, quantifiers, implication, counterexample, induction and proof structure. |
| `TS-F02` | Calculus + vector calculus | Own limits, derivatives as local linearization, integrals as accumulation, gradients/Jacobians, line/surface integrals and field theorems. |
| `TS-F03` | Linear algebra | Own vector spaces, projections, least squares, matrix maps, rank/nullspace, conditioning, SVD and eigenstructure. |
| `TS-F04` | ODEs + dynamical systems | Translate changing systems into state equations; solve and analyze equilibria, stability, oscillation, nonlinear behavior and feedback. |
| `TS-F05` | Probability | Quantify uncertainty, conditioning, expectation, dependence, transformations, concentration and asymptotics. |
| `TS-F06` | Statistics + causal inference | Define estimands; separate prediction from intervention; identify confounding, selection and measurement bias; estimate and stress-test effects. |
| `TS-F07` | Scientific computing | Know conditioning, numerical stability, approximation, iterative solution, ODE/PDE discretization and computational verification. |
| `TS-F08` | Optimization | Formulate objectives and constraints; understand convexity, duality, KKT conditions and numerical optimization; audit objectives. |
| `TS-F09` | Information + signals | Own entropy, mutual information, channel capacity, transforms, sampling, filtering, spectral reasoning and estimation limits. |
| `TS-F10` | Control + estimation | Design closed loops; analyze stability and robustness; infer hidden state; reason about controllability, observability and tradeoffs. |
| `TS-F11` | Metrology + experiments | Calibrate, quantify uncertainty, design informative experiments, distinguish process variation from measurement variation and reproduce results. |
| `TS-F12` | Mechanics + electromagnetism | Convert forces, fields and conservation laws into predictive models; move between particle, rigid-body, orbital and field descriptions. |
| `TS-F13` | Thermodynamics + statistical mechanics | Track energy, entropy and free energy; derive equilibrium tendencies; connect microscopic states to macroscopic limits and transport. |
| `TS-F14` | Quantum + condensed matter | Reason with wavefunctions/operators and connect quantum states to bands, semiconductors, phonons, magnetism and superconductivity. |
| `TS-F15` | Chemistry + materials | Predict bonding, reaction direction/rate, electrochemistry, diffusion, phase behavior and processing-structure-property links. |
| `TS-F16` | Electronics + embedded systems | Design and instrument circuits from passive networks through semiconductor interfaces, sensing, power conversion and real-time embedded control. |
| `TS-F17` | Mechanical design + fluids + structures | Turn loads and flows into safe geometry; reason about stress, fatigue, shells, fluids, pumps, pressure systems and mass-efficient structures. |
| `TS-F18` | Molecular + cell biology | Model cells as chemical, energetic, informational and mechanical systems; understand membranes, metabolism, signaling, division and gene expression. |
| `TS-F19` | Genetics + evolution + systems biology | Reason about inheritance, variation, population change, gene circuits, network motifs, stochastic expression and evolutionary robustness. |
| `TS-F20` | Neuroscience + physiology | Connect electrophysiology and neural coding to whole-body homeostasis and cardiovascular, respiratory, endocrine and renal control. |
| `TS-F21` | Manufacturing + operations | Translate prototypes into processes with rate, yield, variation, quality, cost, maintenance, supply chain and learning curves. |
| `TS-F22` | Safety + reliability + security | Design systems that remain safe and dependable under component failure, software defects, organizational drift, misuse and adversaries. |
