# Mind, Learning & Agency

Attention, memory, practice, mental models, expertise, metacognition, motivation, emotion, embodied skill, creativity, social learning, development, judgment and human-AI cognition.

**Imported editorial label:** `candidate`. This is not a current evidence grade.
See [publication status and evidence](../../project/evidence.md).

## Deletion test

The architecture lacks an operational theory of how humans acquire, retain, transfer, regulate and renew competence.

## Modules

| ID | Module | Exit capability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ML01` | Learning as a Measurable System | Define a learnable capability, baseline it, choose evidence, and distinguish performance today from retained transferable learning. |

| `ML07` | Metacognition, Calibration & Error Monitoring | Track what you know, do not know, and which strategy should change after evidence. |
| `ML08` | Motivation, Goals, Habits & Self-Regulation | Build routines that sustain difficult learning and revise goals without self-deception. |
| `ML09` | Emotion, Stress, Sleep & Performance | Recognize how state changes cognition and design work/learning around physiological constraints. |
| `ML10` | Perception, Motor Learning & Embodied Skill | Acquire and refine a physical skill through observation, practice, feedback, and adaptation. |
| `ML11` | Creativity, Analogy, Exploration & Problem Finding | Generate diverse possibilities, identify promising anomalies, and choose questions worth pursuing. |

| `ML13` | Development, Identity & Lifespan Learning | Adapt learning environments and expectations to developmental stage, identity, culture, and changing capacity. |
| `ML14` | Judgment Under Bounded Cognition | Predict recurring human judgment failures and design checks that outperform willpower alone. |
| `ML15` | Human–AI Cognition, Trust & Delegation | Use AI to extend thought while preserving learning, ownership, and calibrated trust. |
