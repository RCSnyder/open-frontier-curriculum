# Project language and conceptual lexicon

No single inherited word exactly describes the undertaking. Each captures one facet.

## Recommended formal description

> **Open Frontier Curriculum: an open civilizational reference architecture for lifelong human formation in an age of abundant machine intelligence.**

A useful shorter definition is:

> **A living inheritance-and-competence system: what humans should know, practice, become and remain responsible for.**

## Why “Obelisk” can work

An obelisk is durable, visible and orienting. That fits the aspiration to build something more lasting than a tool-specific syllabus. The danger is that an obelisk sounds static or monolithic. Open Frontier should therefore always pair the metaphor with **living, open, versioned, adversarially maintained**.

## Neighboring concepts

- **Curriculum**  -  the sequence of study.
- **Canon**  -  the selected inheritance worth serious encounter.
- **Corpus**  -  the body of sources.
- **Compendium / encyclopedia**  -  organized breadth of knowledge.
- **Atlas**  -  a navigational map through a large intellectual territory.
- **Knowledge graph**  -  the machine-addressable structure of concepts and relations.
- **Reference architecture**  -  stable components, interfaces, invariants and change rules.
- **Constitution**  -  the governing principles that constrain what can be added, removed or claimed.
- **Paideia**  -  whole-person and civic formation through culture, reason and practice.
- **Bildung**  -  reflective self-cultivation through knowledge and culture.
- **Liberal education**  -  formation for judgment and freedom beyond narrow vocation.
- **Competence framework**  -  observable capabilities and standards.
- **Living canon**  -  inherited material that remains selectable, criticizable and revisable.
- **Knowledge commons**  -  openly stewarded intellectual infrastructure.
- **Civilizational inheritance**  -  accumulated discoveries, works, institutions, arguments and failures transmitted across generations.
- **Metacurriculum**  -  the rules and evidence by which the curriculum itself is constructed and revised.
- **Human formation constitution**  -  the first principles governing what education is trying to form and protect.

## Phrases that fit the ambition

Depending on context, the Obelisk can accurately be described as a:

- **civilizational reference architecture**;
- **living canon of human formation**;
- **open paideia**;
- **human capability architecture**;
- **competence constitution**;
- **knowledge-and-formation commons**;
- **lifelong general education architecture**;
- **polymathic formation system**;
- **civilizational knowledge graph**;
- **human authorship curriculum**;
- **stewardship curriculum**;
- **reference canon for broad human competence**;
- **open-source architecture of human intellectual and practical formation**.

None of these should replace the actual definition. They are lenses on the same project.

## Recommended motto

> **Inherit deeply. Understand first principles. Judge independently. Create with taste. Act competently. Relate humanely. Steward wisely. Learn forever.**
