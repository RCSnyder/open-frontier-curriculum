## What “reference-grade shelf” means

For a mature module, editors should normally consider the roles in `reading-architecture.json`: a coherent spine, primary/original source where meaningful, a serious rival or counterpoint, historical predecessor where useful, modern synthesis, real case/failure, global counter-case where needed, data/object/work, and a professional reference. Not every role is appropriate to every module. The rule is **fit to the epistemology of the field**, not mechanical source counting.

`source-stack-audit.json` records the current direct/inherited source state target by target.

## Freeze rule

Source coverage alone cannot freeze a branch. Philosophy has an external hold-out. Human Worlds, Expression, Mind/Learning, and Design still require frozen independent external hold-outs collected after the graph version is fixed.
