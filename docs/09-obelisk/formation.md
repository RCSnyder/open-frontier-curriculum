# Formation and AI modes

Reading is indispensable. Reading alone is not formation.

## Formation modes

### R | Read / Encounter

Primary sources, rigorous textbooks, data, artifacts, works of art, historical documents, field observation.

**Evidence:** Annotated reading/field ledger

### S | Study / Reconstruct

Derive, diagram, explain, retrieve, solve, compare, build concept maps, reproduce examples without aid.

**Evidence:** Closed-book reconstruction

### P | Practice / Drill

Repeated exercises with feedback, spacing, interleaving, increasing difficulty, error taxonomy.

**Evidence:** Practice log + performance curve

### M | Make / Investigate

Experiments, code, essays, designs, models, performances, repairs, interviews, research.

**Evidence:** Inspectible artifact + process record

### D | Dialogue / Critique

Seminar, Socratic questioning, peer review, debate, listening, design critique, negotiation.

**Evidence:** Discussion/critique record

### A | Apply / Serve

Use knowledge for a real person, institution, community, field site, or operational system.

**Evidence:** Stakeholder/field evidence

### B | Break / Red-team

Construct counterexamples, edge cases, adversarial prompts, failure injections, alternative interpretations.

**Evidence:** Failure catalogue

### C | Compare / Curate

Rank alternatives, study expert exemplars, defend criteria, revise taste.

**Evidence:** Blind ranking + curation statement

### F | Forecast / Decide

Make probabilistic forecasts and consequential decisions before outcomes are known.

**Evidence:** Scored forecast / decision journal

### T | Teach / Translate

Teach a novice, translate across disciplines/languages/media, create worked examples.

**Evidence:** Learner pre/post or audience evidence

### V | Viva / Defend

Oral defense, perturbation, hostile but fair questioning, identify unresolved uncertainty.

**Evidence:** Examiner record

### L | Longitudinal Retest

Return weeks/months later, often without AI or notes, and solve a changed problem.

**Evidence:** Delayed score / transfer artifact

## AI modes

### A0 | Human-only acquisition

No general-purpose AI during first reconstruction.

**Purpose:** Protect initial encoding, mental models, fluency, and error detection.

**Pass condition:** Fail if learner cannot explain from memory at required granularity.

### A1 | AI as tutor / questioner

AI may ask questions, generate practice, give bounded hints, or simulate an examiner.

**Purpose:** Use AI to increase deliberate practice, not replace it.

**Pass condition:** Pass only if gains persist without AI.

### A2 | AI as copilot

AI may draft alternatives, code, search, simulate, translate, or critique.

**Purpose:** Learn augmentation and comparative judgment.

**Pass condition:** Polish alone receives no credit.

### A3 | AI as delegated agent

AI may execute multi-step work with tools under permissions.

**Purpose:** Learn orchestration, monitoring, and exception handling.

**Pass condition:** Learner must predict likely failure classes before run.

### A4 | AI as adversary / red team

AI deliberately attacks claims, designs, incentives, security, and reasoning.

**Purpose:** Train robustness and epistemic humility.

**Pass condition:** Pass requires changing something important when attack succeeds.

### A5 | Human defense / fallback

AI removed; learner defends decisions, key models, evidence, and uncertainty.

**Purpose:** Ensure the person owns the result rather than merely operating a system.

**Pass condition:** Pass only if learner can distinguish what they know from what the AI supplied.
