# Thermodynamics + statistical mechanics

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | An Introduction to Thermal Physics
**Daniel V. Schroeder** | Primary spine | Undergraduate

Best first serious thermal text for entropy, free energy, chemical potential, partition functions, quantum statistics, and real physical intuition.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=An+Introduction+to+Thermal+Physics+Daniel+V.+Schroeder) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Thermodynamics and an Introduction to Thermostatistics
**Herbert B. Callen** | Thermodynamic structure | Graduate / advanced undergraduate

Axiomatic, elegant account of thermodynamics, equilibrium, potentials, stability, and Legendre transforms.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Thermodynamics+and+an+Introduction+to+Thermostatistics+Herbert+B.+Callen) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Fundamentals of Statistical and Thermal Physics
**Frederick Reif** | Statistical mechanics depth | Upper undergraduate

Classic text connecting microscopic statistics to macroscopic thermodynamics with careful derivations.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Fundamentals+of+Statistical+and+Thermal+Physics+Frederick+Reif) | No legal full text located

## 4 | Thermal Physics
**Charles Kittel, Herbert Kroemer** | Compact advanced text | Upper undergraduate

Dense, elegant treatment of entropy, temperature, chemical potential, and statistical mechanics.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Thermal+Physics+Charles+Kittel%2C+Herbert+Kroemer) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Statistical Mechanics
**R. K. Pathria, Paul D. Beale** | Graduate reference | Graduate

Comprehensive graduate reference for ensembles, quantum statistics, phase transitions, and interacting systems.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Statistical+Mechanics+R.+K.+Pathria%2C+Paul+D.+Beale) | No legal full text located
