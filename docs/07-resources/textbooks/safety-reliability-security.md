# Safety + reliability + security

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | An Introduction to System Safety Engineering
**Nancy G. Leveson** | Safety primary spine | Upper undergraduate / graduate

Modern system-safety foundation centered on hazards, constraints, accidents, STPA/STAMP, and sociotechnical causality.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=An+Introduction+to+System+Safety+Engineering+Nancy+G.+Leveson) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Engineering a Safer World: Systems Thinking Applied to Safety
**Nancy G. Leveson** | Open systems-safety depth | Graduate / professional

Landmark systems-theoretic account of accident causation, control structures, organizational factors, and safety constraints.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Engineering+a+Safer+World%3A+Systems+Thinking+Applied+to+Safety+Nancy+G.+Leveson) | [Full open text](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262016629/engineering-a-safer-world/)

## 3 | Practical Reliability Engineering
**Patrick D. T. O'Connor, Andre Kleyner** | Reliability spine | Professional / upper undergraduate

Applied reliability prediction, testing, failure data, maintainability, FMEA, reliability growth, and life-cycle methods.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Practical+Reliability+Engineering+Patrick+D.+T.+O%27Connor%2C+Andre+Kleyner) | No legal full text located

## 4 | Risk Analysis in Engineering: Techniques, Tools, and Trends
**Mohammad Modarres** | Risk analysis | Graduate / professional

Quantitative risk, uncertainty, fault/event trees, common-cause failure, and decision support.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Risk+Analysis+in+Engineering%3A+Techniques%2C+Tools%2C+and+Trends+Mohammad+Modarres) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Security Engineering
**Ross Anderson** | Security spine | Upper undergraduate / professional

Deep systems-security reasoning spanning protocols, authentication, hardware, banking, physical systems, economics, and adversaries.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Security+Engineering+Ross+Anderson) | [Author-hosted free editions](https://www.cl.cam.ac.uk/archive/rja14/book.html)
