# Mechanics + electromagnetism

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Classical Mechanics
**John R. Taylor** | Mechanics spine | Undergraduate

Clear route from Newtonian mechanics through oscillations, central forces, noninertial frames, Lagrangian and Hamiltonian methods.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Classical+Mechanics+John+R.+Taylor) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Introduction to Electrodynamics
**David J. Griffiths** | E&M spine | Undergraduate

The standard conceptual route through vector calculus, electrostatics, magnetostatics, Maxwell's equations, waves, radiation, and relativity.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Introduction+to+Electrodynamics+David+J.+Griffiths) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Electricity and Magnetism
**Edward M. Purcell, David J. Morin** | E&M depth / relativity | Undergraduate

Builds field intuition and connects magnetism to relativity unusually well.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Electricity+and+Magnetism+Edward+M.+Purcell%2C+David+J.+Morin) | No legal full text located

## 4 | An Introduction to Mechanics
**Daniel Kleppner, Robert Kolenkow** | Mechanics problem depth | Undergraduate

Challenging mechanics problems that build genuine physical modeling ability.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=An+Introduction+to+Mechanics+Daniel+Kleppner%2C+Robert+Kolenkow) | No legal full text located

## 5 | University Physics, Volumes 1-3
**OpenStax** | Open comprehensive path | Undergraduate

Legally free calculus-based physics sequence covering mechanics, waves, thermo, E&M, optics, relativity, quantum, and modern physics.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=University+Physics%2C+Volumes+1%E2%80%933+OpenStax) | [Full open text](https://openstax.org/details/books/university-physics-volume-1)
