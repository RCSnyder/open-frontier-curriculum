# Information + signals

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Elements of Information Theory
**Thomas M. Cover, Joy A. Thomas** | Information theory spine | Graduate / advanced undergraduate

Canonical treatment of entropy, mutual information, source/channel coding, typicality, rate-distortion, and network information.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Elements+of+Information+Theory+Thomas+M.+Cover%2C+Joy+A.+Thomas) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Signals and Systems
**Alan V. Oppenheim, Alan S. Willsky, S. Hamid Nawab** | Signals spine | Undergraduate

Core continuous/discrete LTI thinking, convolution, Fourier/Laplace/Z transforms, sampling, feedback, and state-space.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Signals+and+Systems+Alan+V.+Oppenheim%2C+Alan+S.+Willsky%2C+S.+Hamid+Nawab) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Discrete-Time Signal Processing, 3rd ed.
**Alan V. Oppenheim, Ronald W. Schafer** | DSP depth | Upper undergraduate / graduate

Canonical discrete-time filtering, transforms, spectral analysis, and multirate signal processing; full 3rd edition is now on MIT OCW.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Discrete-Time+Signal+Processing%2C+3rd+ed.+Alan+V.+Oppenheim%2C+Ronald+W.+Schafer) | [Full open current edition](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-6-dtsp-discrete-time-signal-processing/)

## 4 | Information Theory, Inference, and Learning Algorithms
**David J. C. MacKay** | Inference bridge | Graduate / advanced undergraduate

Beautiful unification of coding, Bayesian inference, statistical physics, and learning.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Information+Theory%2C+Inference%2C+and+Learning+Algorithms+David+J.+C.+MacKay) | [Full legal author-hosted text](https://www.inference.org.uk/itprnn/book.html)

## 5 | Fundamentals of Statistical Signal Processing, Vol. I: Estimation Theory
**Steven M. Kay** | Estimation reference | Graduate

Core estimation bounds, MLE/MAP, linear models, Bayesian estimators, and signal-parameter inference.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Fundamentals+of+Statistical+Signal+Processing%2C+Vol.+I%3A+Estimation+Theory+Steven+M.+Kay) | No legal full text located
