# PDEs + transport

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Partial Differential Equations: An Introduction
**Walter A. Strauss** | Primary spine | Upper undergraduate

Excellent first serious treatment of waves, diffusion, Laplace equations, Fourier methods, and characteristics.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Partial+Differential+Equations%3A+An+Introduction+Walter+A.+Strauss) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Partial Differential Equations
**Lawrence C. Evans** | Rigor / reference | Graduate

The modern graduate standard for weak solutions, Sobolev methods, elliptic/parabolic/hyperbolic PDEs, and nonlinear PDEs.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Partial+Differential+Equations+Lawrence+C.+Evans) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Applied Partial Differential Equations with Fourier Series and Boundary Value Problems
**Richard Haberman** | Applications | Undergraduate

Strong engineering/science treatment of separation of variables, transforms, and physical boundary-value problems.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Applied+Partial+Differential+Equations+with+Fourier+Series+and+Boundary+Value+Problems+Richard+Haberman) | No legal full text located

## 4 | Applied Partial Differential Equations
**J. David Logan** | Modeling | Undergraduate / graduate

Connects PDE forms to transport, conservation, diffusion, waves, and real applied models.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Applied+Partial+Differential+Equations+J.+David+Logan) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Partial Differential Equations for Scientists and Engineers
**Stanley J. Farlow** | Problem-solving | Undergraduate

Compact and practical introduction to the major PDE families and classical solution techniques.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Partial+Differential+Equations+for+Scientists+and+Engineers+Stanley+J.+Farlow) | No legal full text located
