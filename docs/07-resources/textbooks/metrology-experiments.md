# Metrology + experiments

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods
**NIST, SEMATECH** | Primary reference | Undergraduate / professional

Authoritative practical reference for measurement, uncertainty, exploratory analysis, calibration, DOE, reliability, and process statistics.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=NIST%2FSEMATECH+e-Handbook+of+Statistical+Methods+NIST%2C+SEMATECH) | [Full legal online handbook](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/)

## 2 | Design and Analysis of Experiments
**Douglas C. Montgomery** | DOE spine | Upper undergraduate / graduate

The standard engineering text for factorials, blocking, response surfaces, robust experiments, and statistical design.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Design+and+Analysis+of+Experiments+Douglas+C.+Montgomery) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Statistics for Experimenters: Design, Innovation, and Discovery
**George E. P. Box, J. Stuart Hunter, William G. Hunter** | Experimental thinking | Graduate / professional

Develops sequential experimentation, factorial reasoning, response surfaces, and the philosophy of learning from intervention.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Statistics+for+Experimenters%3A+Design%2C+Innovation%2C+and+Discovery+George+E.+P.+Box%2C+J.+Stuart+Hunter%2C+William+G.+Hunter) | No legal full text located

## 4 | An Introduction to Error Analysis
**John R. Taylor** | Measurement uncertainty | Undergraduate

Best compact introduction to uncertainty, propagation, distributions, fits, and reporting physical measurements.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=An+Introduction+to+Error+Analysis+John+R.+Taylor) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Data Reduction and Error Analysis for the Physical Sciences
**Philip R. Bevington, D. Keith Robinson** | Data-analysis depth | Undergraduate / graduate

Classic scientific-data treatment of uncertainties, fitting, likelihood, regression, and computational analysis.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Data+Reduction+and+Error+Analysis+for+the+Physical+Sciences+Philip+R.+Bevington%2C+D.+Keith+Robinson) | No legal full text located

## Strongest legal free/open path

- **NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods**: Full legal online handbook: [open](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/)
