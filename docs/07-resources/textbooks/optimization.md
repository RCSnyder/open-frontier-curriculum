# Optimization

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Convex Optimization
**Stephen Boyd, Lieven Vandenberghe** | Primary spine | Upper undergraduate / graduate

The canonical modern text for convexity, duality, KKT conditions, modeling, algorithms, and engineering applications.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Convex+Optimization+Stephen+Boyd%2C+Lieven+Vandenberghe) | [Full legal web/PDF](https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/)

## 2 | Numerical Optimization
**Jorge Nocedal, Stephen J. Wright** | Algorithms / depth | Graduate

Core reference for line search, trust region, quasi-Newton, constrained methods, and large-scale numerical optimization.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Numerical+Optimization+Jorge+Nocedal%2C+Stephen+J.+Wright) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Nonlinear Programming
**Dimitri P. Bertsekas** | Nonlinear depth | Graduate

Deep treatment of constrained nonlinear optimization, duality, and algorithmic convergence.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Nonlinear+Programming+Dimitri+P.+Bertsekas) | No legal full text located

## 4 | Introduction to Linear Optimization
**Dimitris Bertsimas, John N. Tsitsiklis** | Linear / combinatorial foundation | Upper undergraduate / graduate

Excellent foundations in LP, duality, simplex/interior methods, and discrete optimization thinking.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Introduction+to+Linear+Optimization+Dimitris+Bertsimas%2C+John+N.+Tsitsiklis) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Algorithms for Optimization
**Mykel J. Kochenderfer, Tim A. Wheeler** | Open computational path | Undergraduate / graduate

Broad, implementation-oriented survey from gradient methods to stochastic and global optimization.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Algorithms+for+Optimization+Mykel+J.+Kochenderfer%2C+Tim+A.+Wheeler) | [Full legal PDF / web](https://algorithmsbook.com/optimization/)
