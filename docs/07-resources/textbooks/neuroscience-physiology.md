# Neuroscience + physiology

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Neuroscience
**Dale Purves et al.** | Neuroscience primary spine | Undergraduate

Broad, readable systems neuroscience from cells and synapses to sensation, action, development, and cognition.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Neuroscience+Dale+Purves+et+al.) | No legal current full text located

## 2 | Principles of Neural Science
**Eric R. Kandel et al.** | Deep reference | Graduate / medical

The enormous canonical reference across cellular, systems, cognitive, developmental, and clinical neuroscience.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Principles+of+Neural+Science+Eric+R.+Kandel+et+al.) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Neuroscience: Exploring the Brain
**Mark F. Bear, Barry W. Connors, Michael A. Paradiso** | Undergraduate alternate | Undergraduate

Strong explanatory text with particularly good sensory, motor, plasticity, and systems treatment.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Neuroscience%3A+Exploring+the+Brain+Mark+F.+Bear%2C+Barry+W.+Connors%2C+Michael+A.+Paradiso) | No legal full text located

## 4 | Neuronal Dynamics: From Single Neurons to Networks and Models of Cognition
**Wulfram Gerstner, Werner M. Kistler, Richard Naud, Liam Paninski** | Computational neuroscience | Upper undergraduate / graduate

Excellent mathematical bridge from spiking neurons through population dynamics, plasticity, decoding, and decision models.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Neuronal+Dynamics%3A+From+Single+Neurons+to+Networks+and+Models+of+Cognition+Wulfram+Gerstner%2C+Werner+M.+Kistler%2C+Richard+Naud%2C+Liam+Paninski) | [Full legal online text](https://neuronaldynamics.epfl.ch/online/index.html)

## 5 | Human Physiology: An Integrated Approach
**Dee Unglaub Silverthorn** | Physiology spine | Undergraduate

System-level physiology with especially strong homeostasis and integrative-regulation framing.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Human+Physiology%3A+An+Integrated+Approach+Dee+Unglaub+Silverthorn) | No legal full text located
