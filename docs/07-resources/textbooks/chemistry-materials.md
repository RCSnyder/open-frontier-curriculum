# Chemistry + materials

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Materials Science and Engineering: An Introduction
**William D. Callister Jr., David G. Rethwisch** | Materials primary spine | Undergraduate

The standard processing-structure-properties-performance foundation across metals, ceramics, polymers, composites, and electronic materials.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Materials+Science+and+Engineering%3A+An+Introduction+William+D.+Callister+Jr.%2C+David+G.+Rethwisch) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Engineering Materials 1: An Introduction to Properties, Applications and Design
**Michael F. Ashby, David R. H. Jones** | Engineering intuition | Undergraduate

Builds transferable intuition for stiffness, strength, fracture, fatigue, creep, and material selection.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Engineering+Materials+1%3A+An+Introduction+to+Properties%2C+Applications+and+Design+Michael+F.+Ashby%2C+David+R.+H.+Jones) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Materials Selection in Mechanical Design
**Michael F. Ashby** | Design / selection | Upper undergraduate / professional

Turns materials knowledge into a quantitative design process using property charts, objectives, and constraints.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Materials+Selection+in+Mechanical+Design+Michael+F.+Ashby) | No legal full text located

## 4 | Atkins' Physical Chemistry
**Peter Atkins, Julio de Paula, James Keeler** | Chemistry depth | Undergraduate / graduate

Core thermodynamics, kinetics, quantum chemistry, spectroscopy, and molecular statistical mechanics.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Atkins%27+Physical+Chemistry+Peter+Atkins%2C+Julio+de+Paula%2C+James+Keeler) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Chemistry 2e
**OpenStax** | Open chemistry spine | Undergraduate

Full legal general-chemistry text covering atomic/molecular structure, thermochemistry, equilibrium, kinetics, electrochemistry, and more.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Chemistry+2e+OpenStax) | [Full open text](https://openstax.org/details/books/chemistry-2e)
