# Economics + institutions

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Intermediate Microeconomics: A Modern Approach
**Hal R. Varian** | Microeconomics spine | Undergraduate

Core choice, incentives, demand, production, markets, uncertainty, game theory, information, and welfare.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Intermediate+Microeconomics%3A+A+Modern+Approach+Hal+R.+Varian) | No legal full text located

## 2 | The Economy 2.0: Microeconomics
**CORE Econ** | Open economics spine | Undergraduate

Modern, empirically grounded and legally free microeconomics with institutions, power, inequality, markets, externalities, and policy.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=The+Economy+2.0%3A+Microeconomics+CORE+Econ) | [Full open text](https://www.core-econ.org/ebooks/)

## 3 | Governing the Commons: The Evolution of Institutions for Collective Action
**Elinor Ostrom** | Institutional governance | Upper undergraduate / graduate

Foundational empirical theory of how groups can govern shared resources without reducing every problem to state-vs-market.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Governing+the+Commons%3A+The+Evolution+of+Institutions+for+Collective+Action+Elinor+Ostrom) | No legal full text located

## 4 | The Logic of Political Survival
**Bruce Bueno de Mesquita, Alastair Smith, Randolph M. Siverson, James D. Morrow** | Political incentives | Graduate / advanced undergraduate

A formal theory of political survival, selectorates, coalition size, public/private goods, and leader incentives.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=The+Logic+of+Political+Survival+Bruce+Bueno+de+Mesquita%2C+Alastair+Smith%2C+Randolph+M.+Siverson%2C+James+D.+Morrow) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Exit, Voice, and Loyalty
**Albert O. Hirschman** | Institutional response | Undergraduate / graduate

Small but powerful model of organizational decline and member responses: leave, protest, remain loyal, or combinations thereof.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Exit%2C+Voice%2C+and+Loyalty+Albert+O.+Hirschman) | No legal full text located
