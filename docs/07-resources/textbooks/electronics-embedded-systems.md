# Electronics + embedded systems

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | The Art of Electronics
**Paul Horowitz, Winfield Hill** | Electronics primary reference | Undergraduate / professional

Unmatched practical intuition for analog/digital electronics, devices, op-amps, noise, power, interfaces, and instrumentation.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=The+Art+of+Electronics+Paul+Horowitz%2C+Winfield+Hill) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Microelectronic Circuits
**Adel S. Sedra, Kenneth C. Smith** | Circuit theory spine | Undergraduate

Standard rigorous electronics text for semiconductor devices, amplifiers, op-amps, filters, feedback, and digital circuits.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Microelectronic+Circuits+Adel+S.+Sedra%2C+Kenneth+C.+Smith) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Fundamentals of Microelectronics
**Behzad Razavi** | Device-to-circuit intuition | Undergraduate

Exceptionally clear semiconductor and analog-circuit explanations with strong design intuition.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Fundamentals+of+Microelectronics+Behzad+Razavi) | No legal full text located

## 4 | Embedded Systems: Real-Time Interfacing to Arm Cortex-M Microcontrollers
**Jonathan W. Valvano** | Embedded spine | Undergraduate

Hardware/software co-design, interrupts, timing, ADC/DAC, serial interfaces, real-time systems, and microcontroller projects.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Embedded+Systems%3A+Real-Time+Interfacing+to+Arm+Cortex-M+Microcontrollers+Jonathan+W.+Valvano) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Making Embedded Systems: Design Patterns for Great Software
**Elecia White** | Industry practice | Professional / undergraduate

Real embedded-engineering concerns: architecture, peripherals, debugging, resource limits, state machines, and team design.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Making+Embedded+Systems%3A+Design+Patterns+for+Great+Software+Elecia+White) | No legal full text located
