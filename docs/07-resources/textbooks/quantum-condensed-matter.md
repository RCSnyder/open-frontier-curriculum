# Quantum + condensed matter

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Introduction to Quantum Mechanics
**David J. Griffiths, Darrell F. Schroeter** | Quantum primary spine | Undergraduate

Standard first serious quantum text: wave mechanics, formalism, angular momentum, identical particles, perturbation, scattering.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Introduction+to+Quantum+Mechanics+David+J.+Griffiths%2C+Darrell+F.+Schroeter) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Principles of Quantum Mechanics
**R. Shankar** | Conceptual / mathematical depth | Upper undergraduate / graduate

Deep development of Hilbert-space formalism with substantial classical-mechanics and mathematical preparation.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Principles+of+Quantum+Mechanics+R.+Shankar) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Modern Quantum Mechanics
**J. J. Sakurai, Jim Napolitano** | Graduate quantum | Graduate

Canonical graduate route into symmetry, angular momentum, approximation, scattering, and relativistic quantum mechanics.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Modern+Quantum+Mechanics+J.+J.+Sakurai%2C+Jim+Napolitano) | No legal full text located

## 4 | Introduction to Solid State Physics
**Charles Kittel** | Condensed-matter spine | Upper undergraduate

Classic entry into crystal structures, phonons, electrons, semiconductors, magnetism, superconductivity, and solids.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Introduction+to+Solid+State+Physics+Charles+Kittel) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Solid State Physics
**Neil W. Ashcroft, N. David Mermin** | Condensed-matter depth | Graduate / advanced undergraduate

The enduring deep reference for electrons in solids, band structure, lattice dynamics, transport, magnetism, and Fermi liquids.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Solid+State+Physics+Neil+W.+Ashcroft%2C+N.+David+Mermin) | No legal full text located
