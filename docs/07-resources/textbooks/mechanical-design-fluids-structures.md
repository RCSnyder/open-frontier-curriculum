# Mechanical design + fluids + structures

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Shigley's Mechanical Engineering Design
**Richard G. Budynas, J. Keith Nisbett** | Mechanical design spine | Undergraduate

Core machine-element design: stress, fatigue, shafts, gears, bearings, fasteners, springs, and design reliability.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Shigley%27s+Mechanical+Engineering+Design+Richard+G.+Budynas%2C+J.+Keith+Nisbett) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Mechanics of Materials
**R. C. Hibbeler** | Structures / stress spine | Undergraduate

Clear foundation in stress, strain, torsion, bending, shear, deflection, buckling, and failure.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Mechanics+of+Materials+R.+C.+Hibbeler) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Fluid Mechanics
**Frank M. White** | Fluids spine | Undergraduate

Broad engineering fluids: control-volume analysis, dimensional analysis, viscous flow, boundary layers, compressible flow, turbomachinery.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Fluid+Mechanics+Frank+M.+White) | No legal full text located

## 4 | Fluid Mechanics: Fundamentals and Applications
**Yunus A. Çengel, John M. Cimbala** | Applied fluids | Undergraduate

Accessible engineering treatment with extensive applications, energy equations, pipe flow, external flow, and turbomachinery.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Fluid+Mechanics%3A+Fundamentals+and+Applications+Yunus+A.+%C3%87engel%2C+John+M.+Cimbala) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Introduction to Aerospace Structures and Materials
**René Alderliesten** | Open structures/materials bridge | Undergraduate

Legally open TU Delft text integrating loads, stress/strain, beams, structural concepts, fatigue, and aerospace materials.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Introduction+to+Aerospace+Structures+and+Materials+Ren%C3%A9+Alderliesten) | [Full open text](https://books.open.tudelft.nl/home/catalog/book/197)
