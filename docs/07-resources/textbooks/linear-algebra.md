# Linear algebra

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Introduction to Linear Algebra
**Gilbert Strang** | Primary spine | Undergraduate

Geometric, computational, and applications-oriented; unusually good bridge into engineering, ML, inverse problems, and numerical methods.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Introduction+to+Linear+Algebra+Gilbert+Strang) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Linear Algebra Done Right, 4th ed.
**Sheldon Axler** | Rigor / second pass | Upper undergraduate

Makes invariant subspaces, operators, eigenstructure, and spectral thinking conceptually clean.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Linear+Algebra+Done+Right%2C+4th+ed.+Sheldon+Axler) | [Full open current edition](https://linear.axler.net/)

## 3 | Introduction to Applied Linear Algebra: Vectors, Matrices, and Least Squares
**Stephen Boyd, Lieven Vandenberghe** | Applied bridge | Undergraduate

Excellent for least squares, modeling, data, estimation, and optimization; directly useful in modern engineering.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Introduction+to+Applied+Linear+Algebra%3A+Vectors%2C+Matrices%2C+and+Least+Squares+Stephen+Boyd%2C+Lieven+Vandenberghe) | [Full legal web/PDF](https://stanford.edu/~boyd/vmls/)

## 4 | Linear Algebra
**Jim Hefferon** | Open drill | Undergraduate

Proof-capable first course with extensive worked solutions and computational applications.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Linear+Algebra+Jim+Hefferon) | [Full open text](https://hefferon.net/linearalgebra/)

## 5 | A First Course in Linear Algebra
**Robert A. Beezer** | Open reference | Undergraduate

Broad, formal, and exercise-rich; valuable as a second source when one explanation does not click.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=A+First+Course+in+Linear+Algebra+Robert+A.+Beezer) | [Full open text](https://linear.ups.edu/)
