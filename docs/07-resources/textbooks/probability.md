# Probability

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Introduction to Probability
**Joseph K. Blitzstein, Jessica Hwang** | Primary spine | Undergraduate

Exceptional conceptual training in conditioning, counting, random variables, expectation, and probabilistic thinking.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Introduction+to+Probability+Joseph+K.+Blitzstein%2C+Jessica+Hwang) | [Free legal online materials / edition](https://probabilitybook.net/)

## 2 | Introduction to Probability
**Dimitri P. Bertsekas, John N. Tsitsiklis** | Engineering rigor | Undergraduate

Compact, precise, and especially good for conditional probability, random processes, and engineering applications.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Introduction+to+Probability+Dimitri+P.+Bertsekas%2C+John+N.+Tsitsiklis) | No legal full text located

## 3 | A First Course in Probability
**Sheldon Ross** | Exercise bank | Undergraduate

Large, standard problem repertoire; useful for acquiring speed and fluency.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=A+First+Course+in+Probability+Sheldon+Ross) | No legal full text located

## 4 | Probability and Random Processes
**Geoffrey Grimmett, David Stirzaker** | Depth | Upper undergraduate

Strong transition from elementary probability into stochastic processes and rigorous probabilistic modeling.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Probability+and+Random+Processes+Geoffrey+Grimmett%2C+David+Stirzaker) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Introduction to Probability
**Charles M. Grinstead, J. Laurie Snell** | Open classic | Undergraduate

Classic free text with simulation-flavored intuition and many exercises.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Introduction+to+Probability+Charles+M.+Grinstead%2C+J.+Laurie+Snell) | [Full open text](https://math.dartmouth.edu/~prob/prob/prob.pdf)

## Strongest legal free/open path

- **Introduction to Probability**: Free legal online materials / edition: [open](https://probabilitybook.net/)
- **Introduction to Probability**: Full open text: [open](https://math.dartmouth.edu/~prob/prob/prob.pdf)
