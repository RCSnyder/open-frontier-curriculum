# History, power + human judgment

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | The Blackwell Encyclopaedia of Political Thought
**David Miller, Janet Coleman, William Connolly, Alan Ryan (eds.)** | Political-thought reference | Undergraduate / graduate

Broad reference across political concepts and traditions; useful precisely because no single ideological lens is sufficient.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=The+Blackwell+Encyclopaedia+of+Political+Thought+David+Miller%2C+Janet+Coleman%2C+William+Connolly%2C+Alan+Ryan+%28eds.%29) | No legal full text located

## 2 | A People's History of the United States
**Howard Zinn** | Counter-history lens | Undergraduate

A deliberately oppositional narrative useful for practicing source criticism, missing-actor analysis, and perspective shifts; pair with primary sources and competing histories.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=A+People%27s+History+of+the+United+States+Howard+Zinn) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Design Justice: Community-Led Practices to Build the Worlds We Need
**Sasha Costanza-Chock** | Sociotechnical design | Undergraduate / graduate

Directly relevant to who defines problems, who participates, who benefits, who bears harms, and how design processes encode power.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Design+Justice%3A+Community-Led+Practices+to+Build+the+Worlds+We+Need+Sasha+Costanza-Chock) | [Full open text](https://designjustice.mitpress.mit.edu/)

## 4 | The Roots of Evil: The Origins of Genocide and Other Group Violence
**Ervin Staub** | Violence / bystander psychology | Graduate / advanced undergraduate

Mechanistic account of difficult life conditions, group processes, ideology, bystanders, escalation, and mass violence.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=The+Roots+of+Evil%3A+The+Origins+of+Genocide+and+Other+Group+Violence+Ervin+Staub) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Difficult Conversations: How to Discuss What Matters Most
**Douglas Stone, Bruce Patton, Sheila Heen** | Conflict practice | Undergraduate / professional

Highly usable framework for factual disagreement, identity threat, emotions, contribution, and learning-oriented conflict.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Difficult+Conversations%3A+How+to+Discuss+What+Matters+Most+Douglas+Stone%2C+Bruce+Patton%2C+Sheila+Heen) | No legal full text located
