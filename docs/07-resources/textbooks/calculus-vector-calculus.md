# Calculus + vector calculus

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Calculus, Volumes I-II
**Tom M. Apostol** | Primary rigorous spine | Undergraduate

Treats calculus as a mathematical theory tied to linear algebra and physics, not a bag of tricks.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Calculus%2C+Volumes+I%E2%80%93II+Tom+M.+Apostol) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Calculus
**Michael Spivak** | Proof-oriented depth | Undergraduate

For understanding why single-variable calculus works; develops mathematical maturity.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Calculus+Michael+Spivak) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Vector Calculus, Linear Algebra, and Differential Forms: A Unified Approach
**John H. Hubbard, Barbara Burke Hubbard** | Multivariable / geometry | Upper undergraduate

Unifies derivatives, manifolds, forms, and linear algebra in a way that transfers directly to physics and control.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Vector+Calculus%2C+Linear+Algebra%2C+and+Differential+Forms%3A+A+Unified+Approach+John+H.+Hubbard%2C+Barbara+Burke+Hubbard) | No legal full text located

## 4 | Calculus, Volumes 1-3
**OpenStax** | Open primary | Undergraduate

Complete standard sequence with a legal free web/PDF edition and large problem sets.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Calculus%2C+Volumes+1%E2%80%933+OpenStax) | [Full open text](https://openstax.org/details/books/calculus-volume-1)

## 5 | Active Calculus: Single Variable + Multivariable
**Matthew Boelkins et al.** | Open active-learning path | Undergraduate

Built around doing mathematics, explaining, and solving problems rather than passive reading.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Active+Calculus%3A+Single+Variable+%2B+Multivariable+Matthew+Boelkins+et+al.) | [Full open text](https://activecalculus.org/)

## Strongest legal free/open path

- **Active Calculus: Single Variable + Multivariable**: Full open text: [open](https://activecalculus.org/)
