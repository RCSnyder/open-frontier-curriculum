# Algorithms + software systems

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Introduction to Algorithms
**Thomas H. Cormen, Charles E. Leiserson, Ronald L. Rivest, Clifford Stein** | Algorithms reference | Undergraduate / graduate

The broad reference for data structures, graph algorithms, dynamic programming, greedy methods, randomized algorithms, and complexity.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Introduction+to+Algorithms+Thomas+H.+Cormen%2C+Charles+E.+Leiserson%2C+Ronald+L.+Rivest%2C+Clifford+Stein) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Computer Systems: A Programmer's Perspective
**Randal E. Bryant, David R. O'Hallaron** | Systems spine | Undergraduate

Explains what software actually runs on: machine code, memory, caches, linking, processes, virtual memory, I/O, networking, concurrency.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Computer+Systems%3A+A+Programmer%27s+Perspective+Randal+E.+Bryant%2C+David+R.+O%27Hallaron) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces
**Remzi H. Arpaci-Dusseau, Andrea C. Arpaci-Dusseau** | Open OS spine | Undergraduate

Conceptually clean treatment of virtualization, concurrency, persistence, and security, with projects and simulators.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Operating+Systems%3A+Three+Easy+Pieces+Remzi+H.+Arpaci-Dusseau%2C+Andrea+C.+Arpaci-Dusseau) | [Free legal online chapters](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/)

## 4 | Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs
**Harold Abelson, Gerald Jay Sussman, Julie Sussman** | Abstraction / computation | Undergraduate

Trains compositional thinking, interpreters, abstraction, state, streams, metalinguistic abstraction, and computational models.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Structure+and+Interpretation+of+Computer+Programs+Harold+Abelson%2C+Gerald+Jay+Sussman%2C+Julie+Sussman) | [Full open edition](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262510875/structure-and-interpretation-of-computer-programs/)

## 5 | Designing Data-Intensive Applications
**Martin Kleppmann** | Modern distributed systems | Professional / upper undergraduate

Excellent synthesis of storage, replication, partitioning, streams, consistency, transactions, and distributed-data tradeoffs.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Designing+Data-Intensive+Applications+Martin+Kleppmann) | No legal full text located

## Strongest legal free/open path

- **Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces**: Free legal online chapters: [open](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/)
