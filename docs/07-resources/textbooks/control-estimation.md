# Control + estimation

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Feedback Systems: An Introduction for Scientists and Engineers
**Karl J. Åström, Richard M. Murray** | Primary spine | Upper undergraduate

A superb modern foundation in feedback, state space, frequency response, robustness, stability, design, and biological/engineering examples.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Feedback+Systems%3A+An+Introduction+for+Scientists+and+Engineers+Karl+J.+%C3%85str%C3%B6m%2C+Richard+M.+Murray) | [Full legal web/PDF](https://fbswiki.org/)

## 2 | Modern Control Engineering
**Katsuhiko Ogata** | Classical methods | Undergraduate

Comprehensive classical control text with transfer functions, root locus, frequency methods, state-space design, and examples.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Modern+Control+Engineering+Katsuhiko+Ogata) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Feedback Control of Dynamic Systems
**Gene F. Franklin, J. David Powell, Abbas Emami-Naeini** | Engineering practice | Undergraduate

Strong design-oriented control treatment with realistic engineering examples and system intuition.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Feedback+Control+of+Dynamic+Systems+Gene+F.+Franklin%2C+J.+David+Powell%2C+Abbas+Emami-Naeini) | No legal full text located

## 4 | Optimal State Estimation
**Dan Simon** | Estimation depth | Graduate / professional

A practical deep treatment of Kalman filters, nonlinear estimation, smoothing, and implementation issues.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Optimal+State+Estimation+Dan+Simon) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Applied Nonlinear Control
**Jean-Jacques E. Slotine, Weiping Li** | Nonlinear control | Graduate

Classic route into Lyapunov methods, nonlinear feedback, adaptive control, and robotics-oriented control.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Applied+Nonlinear+Control+Jean-Jacques+E.+Slotine%2C+Weiping+Li) | No legal full text located
