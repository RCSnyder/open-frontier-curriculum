# Manufacturing + operations

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Factory Physics
**Wallace J. Hopp, Mark L. Spearman** | Operations / flow spine | Upper undergraduate / graduate

Essential laws of manufacturing systems: variability, buffers, cycle time, WIP, capacity, push/pull, and flow.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Factory+Physics+Wallace+J.+Hopp%2C+Mark+L.+Spearman) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Fundamentals of Modern Manufacturing: Materials, Processes, and Systems
**Mikell P. Groover** | Manufacturing spine | Undergraduate

Broad manufacturing processes, materials, production systems, quality, automation, and economics.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Fundamentals+of+Modern+Manufacturing%3A+Materials%2C+Processes%2C+and+Systems+Mikell+P.+Groover) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Manufacturing Engineering and Technology
**Serope Kalpakjian, Steven R. Schmid** | Process depth | Undergraduate

Comprehensive process reference from casting/forming/machining to polymers, additive processes, surfaces, quality, and automation.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Manufacturing+Engineering+and+Technology+Serope+Kalpakjian%2C+Steven+R.+Schmid) | No legal full text located

## 4 | Operations Management
**Nigel Slack, Alistair Brandon-Jones, Nicola Burgess** | Operations-management spine | Undergraduate / MBA

Strong framework for capacity, process design, supply networks, quality, inventory, planning, improvement, and operations strategy.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Operations+Management+Nigel+Slack%2C+Alistair+Brandon-Jones%2C+Nicola+Burgess) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Operations Management: Sustainability and Supply Chain Management
**Jay Heizer, Barry Render, Chuck Munson** | Applied operations | Undergraduate

Large applied toolkit for forecasting, capacity, location, inventory, scheduling, quality, supply chain, and project management.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Operations+Management%3A+Sustainability+and+Supply+Chain+Management+Jay+Heizer%2C+Barry+Render%2C+Chuck+Munson) | No legal full text located
