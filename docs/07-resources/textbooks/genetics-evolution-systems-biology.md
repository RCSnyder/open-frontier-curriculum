# Genetics + evolution + systems biology

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Genetics: A Conceptual Approach
**Benjamin A. Pierce** | Genetics primary spine | Undergraduate

Excellent conceptual genetics from Mendel through molecular genetics, genomics, quantitative and population genetics.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Genetics%3A+A+Conceptual+Approach+Benjamin+A.+Pierce) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Principles of Population Genetics
**Daniel L. Hartl, Andrew G. Clark** | Population genetics depth | Upper undergraduate / graduate

Core theory connecting allele frequencies, selection, drift, mutation, migration, linkage, and molecular evolution.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Principles+of+Population+Genetics+Daniel+L.+Hartl%2C+Andrew+G.+Clark) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Evolution
**Douglas J. Futuyma, Mark Kirkpatrick** | Evolution spine | Undergraduate / graduate

Broad modern evolutionary biology with evidence, mechanisms, phylogenetics, adaptation, speciation, and macroevolution.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Evolution+Douglas+J.+Futuyma%2C+Mark+Kirkpatrick) | No legal full text located

## 4 | An Introduction to Systems Biology: Design Principles of Biological Circuits
**Uri Alon** | Systems-biology spine | Upper undergraduate / graduate

Foundational motifs, feedback/feedforward loops, robustness, adaptation, and network design principles.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=An+Introduction+to+Systems+Biology%3A+Design+Principles+of+Biological+Circuits+Uri+Alon) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Evolutionary Dynamics: Exploring the Equations of Life
**Martin A. Nowak** | Mathematical evolution | Upper undergraduate / graduate

Connects evolutionary processes to dynamical systems, games, mutation-selection, spatial structure, and cooperation.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Evolutionary+Dynamics%3A+Exploring+the+Equations+of+Life+Martin+A.+Nowak) | No legal full text located
