# Scientific computing

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Numerical Linear Algebra
**Lloyd N. Trefethen, David Bau III** | Primary spine | Upper undergraduate / graduate

Short, deep, and essential: conditioning, stability, QR, eigenproblems, Krylov methods, and the numerical meaning of linear algebra.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Numerical+Linear+Algebra+Lloyd+N.+Trefethen%2C+David+Bau+III) | No legal full text located

## 2 | Accuracy and Stability of Numerical Algorithms
**Nicholas J. Higham** | Numerical reliability | Graduate

The definitive mindset for rounding error, conditioning, backward error, and why mathematically correct algorithms fail numerically.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Accuracy+and+Stability+of+Numerical+Algorithms+Nicholas+J.+Higham) | No legal full text located

## 3 | Scientific Computing: An Introductory Survey
**Michael T. Heath** | Broad reference | Upper undergraduate

Wide-ranging treatment of numerical algebra, interpolation, integration, ODEs, PDEs, optimization, and computational tradeoffs.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Scientific+Computing%3A+An+Introductory+Survey+Michael+T.+Heath) | No legal full text located

## 4 | Numerical Analysis
**Timothy Sauer** | Methods / practice | Undergraduate

Accessible and comprehensive numerical methods with strong computational orientation.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Numerical+Analysis+Timothy+Sauer) | No legal full text located

## 5 | Fundamentals of Numerical Computation
**Tobin A. Driscoll, Richard J. Braun** | Open computational spine | Undergraduate

Excellent interactive open text spanning conditioning, linear systems, approximation, integration, ODEs, BVPs, and PDEs.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Fundamentals+of+Numerical+Computation+Tobin+A.+Driscoll%2C+Richard+J.+Braun) | [Full open web text](https://fncbook.com/)
