# Statistics + causal inference

Five ranked references. **Rank 1 is the default spine, not a requirement to read all five.**

## 1 | Causal Inference: What If
**Miguel A. Hernán, James M. Robins** | Causal primary spine | Graduate / advanced undergraduate

The most important rigorous bridge from observational data to intervention questions; defines targets, identification, bias, and causal estimation.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Causal+Inference%3A+What+If+Miguel+A.+Hern%C3%A1n%2C+James+M.+Robins) | [Full legal author PDF](https://www.hsph.harvard.edu/miguel-hernan/causal-inference-book/)

## 2 | Regression and Other Stories
**Andrew Gelman, Jennifer Hill, Aki Vehtari** | Applied statistics spine | Undergraduate / graduate

Excellent model-building habits: regression, uncertainty, checking, transformations, and interpretation.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Regression+and+Other+Stories+Andrew+Gelman%2C+Jennifer+Hill%2C+Aki+Vehtari) | No legal full text located

## 3 | All of Statistics: A Concise Course in Statistical Inference
**Larry Wasserman** | Theory compact | Graduate / advanced undergraduate

Dense survey of estimation, testing, regression, Bayesian ideas, bootstrap, density estimation, and learning.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=All+of+Statistics%3A+A+Concise+Course+in+Statistical+Inference+Larry+Wasserman) | No legal full text located

## 4 | The Effect: An Introduction to Research Design and Causality
**Nick Huntington-Klein** | Open causal intuition | Undergraduate

Very strong bridge from identification ideas to real research designs; free web version remains legally available.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=The+Effect%3A+An+Introduction+to+Research+Design+and+Causality+Nick+Huntington-Klein) | [Full legal web edition](https://theeffectbook.net/)

## 5 | Causal Inference: The Mixtape
**Scott Cunningham** | Empirical designs | Undergraduate / graduate

Hands-on treatment of DAGs, regression, matching, IV, diff-in-diff, RDD, and synthetic control.

[Amazon search](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Causal+Inference%3A+The+Mixtape+Scott+Cunningham) | [Full legal web edition](https://mixtape.scunning.com/)
