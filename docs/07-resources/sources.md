# Source notes

| Domain | Source | Institution/author | Epistemic role | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current AI/mathematics | [Mathematics in the age of AI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16753) | Terence Tao | Primary essay / current perspective | Use after building agent systems: interrogates what mathematical research is for if research-level problem solving becomes abundant. |
| AI & mathematics principles | [Leiden Declaration on Artificial Intelligence and Mathematics](https://leidendeclaration.ai/) | Leiden Declaration | Current declaration / normative principles | Use as a human-centered governance position to reconstruct, critique and operationalize-not as empirical proof. |
| Wicked problems | [Dilemmas in a General Theory of Planning](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/fac/ellendo/rittel/rittel-dilemma.pdf) | Horst Rittel, Melvin Webber | Classic planning theory | Use to mark the boundary where objective formulation itself is contested; do not abandon local quantitative optimization. |
| Communication | [What is NVC?](https://www.cnvc.org/learn/what-is-nvc) | Center for Nonviolent Communication | Originating practitioner framework | Use for structured conflict experiments; efficacy claims require separate empirical evidence. |
| Leadership/team practice | [Conscious Leadership resources](https://conscious.is/resources/) | Conscious Leadership Group | Practitioner framework | Use as behavioral experiments in retrospectives; retain only practices that improve accountability/learning. |
| Participatory design | [Design Justice](https://designjustice.mitpress.mit.edu/) | Sasha Costanza-Chock / MIT Press | Open-access scholarship/framework | Use to alter problem-definition and participation mechanisms, paired with technical/effect evidence. |
| System safety | [Engineering a Safer World](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262533690/engineering-a-safer-world/) | Nancy Leveson / MIT Press | Open-access technical monograph | Use as the system-safety anchor for interaction/control/organizational causality. |
| Agent self-improvement | [Darwin Gödel Machine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.22954) | Zhang et al. | Primary research paper | Use to study empirical self-modification with external evaluation/sandbox boundaries. |
| Meta self-improvement | [Hyperagents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19461) | Zhang et al. | Primary research paper (2026) | Use to distinguish task-level improvement from modification of the improvement process itself. |
| Autonomous science | [The AI Scientist-v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08066) | Yamada et al. | Primary research paper | Use for autonomous experiment/research architecture; reproduce and independently verify rather than accepting automated review. |
| Multi-agent systems | [How we built our multi-agent research system](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/multi-agent-research-system) | Anthropic | Engineering report | Useful production evidence on orchestrator-worker patterns, breadth-first tasks, cost and failure; not universal benchmark authority. |
| Space systems | [NASA Systems Engineering Handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/) | NASA Office of the Chief Engineer | Primary/official engineering authority | Use for requirements, architecture, verification/validation and technical management. |
| Space MBSE | [NASA-HDBK-1009A Systems Modeling Handbook](https://standards.nasa.gov/standard/NASA/NASA-HDBK-1009) | NASA | Primary/official current handbook | Active 2025 systems-modeling guidance; use for traceable model-based system artifacts. |
| Space industry | [NASA ISAM](https://etd.gsfc.nasa.gov/capabilities/in-space-servicing-assembly-and-manufacturing/) | NASA Goddard | Primary/official capability program | Use for current in-space servicing/assembly/manufacturing context and staged capability thinking. |
| BCI reality check | [Brain-computer device helps man speak](https://www.nih.gov/news-events/nih-research-matters/brain-computer-device-helps-man-speak) | NIH Research Matters | Primary-authority research summary | Use to anchor neurotech to practical home-use capability and longitudinal utility rather than offline decoding scores. |
