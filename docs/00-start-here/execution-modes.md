# Execution modes

A curriculum week assumes roughly **32 focused hours**. Stretch the calendar rather than deleting mastery gates.

| Mode | Context | Focused hours/week | Estimated calendar time | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Full-time builder | Independent / gap-year / research apprenticeship | 40 | 2.1 years | Fastest; needs access to labs/mentors and unusually strong discipline. |
| High-intensity university overlay | University + canon alignment | 25 | 3.3 years | Use university courses/labs to satisfy reading/build requirements; keep independent mastery gates. |
| Sustainable university overlay | Full-time university | 18 | 4.7 years | Most realistic if the student also completes a degree; summers accelerate Wave 2/3 projects. |
| Working learner | Job/apprenticeship + study | 12 | 7.0 years | Use job artifacts where possible; protect weekly closed-book and build blocks. |
| Compressed elite cohort | Dedicated institute/cohort | 50 | 1.7 years | Possible only with prerequisites, mentoring, labs, strong peer group and aggressive integration. |

## Default 32-hour curriculum week

### Primary reading / source reconstruction: 6h

**Rule:** Read for arguments/models, not highlighting.

**Output:** 1-2 page source map; definitions/assumptions.

### Closed-book regeneration: 5h

**Rule:** No notes/AI until the first serious attempt is complete.

**Output:** Blank-page derivations, proof, model or concept map.

### Transfer problems: 6h

**Rule:** Prefer unseen transfer problems; explain representation before arithmetic.

**Output:** Solved problems + error log.

### Build / simulation / experiment: 8h

**Rule:** Predict before running; measure discrepancy.

**Output:** Code/CAD/data/prototype + test evidence.

### Oral defense / peer review: 2h

**Rule:** Invite attack on assumptions, not performance theater.

**Output:** Recorded defense + revisions.

### AI-assisted acceleration: 3h

**Rule:** Use after ownership attempt for coding, literature comparison, adversarial tests and critique.

**Output:** Prompt/trace + independent verification.

### Reflection / planning: 2h

**Rule:** Update forecast, bottleneck and next experiment.

**Output:** Weekly lab notebook + next-week plan.
