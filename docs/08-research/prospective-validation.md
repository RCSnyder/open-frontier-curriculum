# Prospective validation protocol

Status: protocol, not a completed study. Existing All Souls mappings are preserved
external intellectual proxies. Imported dates and freeze claims are not newly
verified evidence of unseen evaluation.

## Freeze before exposure

Archive the architecture, teaching attachments, source assignments and scoring
rubric with immutable hashes. Record who designed the curriculum and who selected
the evaluation tasks. The selector records prior exposure and keeps task content
outside the public development tree until administration.

Register population, prerequisites, accommodations, safety limits, sampling rules,
exclusions, primary outcomes and stopping criteria before opening the task set.
Distinguish a public benchmark used in development from a genuinely unseen test.
Any accessed prompt is contaminated for that developer and cannot be relabeled.

## Heterogeneous evidence

Use technical reconstruction and failure analysis; PH argument and counterexample;
HW source-packet comparison; EX unseen interpretation and complete-work relations;
ML delayed transfer; DR changed briefs and observed user consequences. Include
practical life tasks only within consent, competence and supervision boundaries.

All Souls specialist and General papers may contribute to some intellectual
dimensions. They do not validate health practice, relational care, craft, ecological
stewardship or longitudinal agency. Link official papers rather than redistributing
copyrighted examination content without permission.

## Administration and scoring

Separate independent acquisition/reconstruction, permitted augmentation and human
defense. Keep tool access and assistance logs. Two qualified examiners score
independently before adjudication, using
[the calibration protocol](../05-formation/examiner-calibration.md).
Record disagreements, accommodations, attrition and invalid administrations.

Report raw denominators and uncertainty, not only average scores. Analyze failure
as missing facts, prerequisite gaps, missing intellectual families, weak instruction,
assessment ambiguity, unsafe practice or access barriers. Do not turn every failure
into a new module or excuse every failure as learner effort.

## Saturation and outcomes

Architecture freeze requires repeated heterogeneous rounds with no new load-bearing
family under a declared decision rule. The rounds must use separately selected
material and cannot be recycled after the curriculum has been changed for it.

Retest retained performance after an agreed delay and in changed contexts. A small
pilot can expose implementation failures; it cannot establish population efficacy.
Comparative or causal claims require an appropriate design and independent review.
Longitudinal functioning remains sensitive to health, opportunity, institutions and
choice. No study can establish a universal scalar maximum of human potential.
