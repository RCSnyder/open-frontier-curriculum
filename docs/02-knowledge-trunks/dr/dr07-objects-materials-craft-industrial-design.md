# DR07  -  Objects, Materials, Craft & Industrial Design

**Branch:** Design & Representation
**Status:** candidate-compression-test
**Prerequisites:** DR01, DR02, DR03

## Core concepts

form/function, ergonomics, tolerance, material, assembly, repair, lifecycle
