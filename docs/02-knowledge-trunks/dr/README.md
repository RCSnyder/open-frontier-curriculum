# Design & Representation

Perception, form, diagrams, visualization, typography, spatial design, objects, interfaces, multimodal composition, aesthetics, prototyping, critique and curation.

## Why this trunk survives deletion

Visual-spatial, interface and aesthetic-functional failures recur even when technical and verbal competence remain intact.

## Modules

| ID | Module | Exit capability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DR01 | Perception, Attention & Affordance | See what a representation or object makes easy, hard, noticeable, or invisible. |
| DR02 | Form, Composition, Hierarchy & Constraint | Organize elements so attention and structure support purpose. |
| DR03 | Drawing, Diagramming & Visual Reasoning | Externalize structure visually and use diagrams to discover errors in thought. |
| DR04 | Data Visualization & Information Design | Choose visual encodings that reveal rather than distort quantity, uncertainty, and comparison. |
| DR05 | Typography, Layout & Document Systems | Create readable documents whose structure survives length, device, and audience. |
| DR06 | Space, Maps, Architecture & Wayfinding | Reason about space and guide human movement through physical or geographic environments. |
| DR07 | Objects, Materials, Craft & Industrial Design | Design an object that fits bodies, materials, manufacturing, maintenance, and use. |
| DR08 | Interaction, Interface & Service Design | Design interactions that preserve agency, visibility, control, and graceful recovery. |
| DR09 | Time, Motion, Sound & Multimodal Composition | Shape experience over time across visual, auditory, and interactive media. |
| DR10 | Aesthetics, Style, History & Global Traditions | Recognize formal lineages and evaluate work without treating one culture's conventions as universal. |
| DR11 | Prototyping, User Research, Critique & Iteration | Turn uncertain assumptions into prototypes and observations; revise without overfitting to one user. |
| DR12 | Curation, Taste & AI-Generated Abundance | Select and improve from hundreds of plausible AI outputs while preserving purpose, originality, and human preference. |
| DR13 | Integrated Design & Representation | Build and defend a coherent information/interface/physical representation system across media. |
