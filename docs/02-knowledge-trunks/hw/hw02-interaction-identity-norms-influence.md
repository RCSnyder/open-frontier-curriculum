# HW02  -  Interaction, Identity, Norms & Influence

**Branch:** Human Worlds
**Status:** candidate-v2
**Prerequisites:** HW01

## Exit capability

Diagnose interpersonal and small-group behavior without reducing it to personality, manipulation, or stated motives.

## Core concepts

roles, framing, norms, status, identity, socialization, persuasion, conformity, face, repair

## Deletion test

Social systems are enacted through interaction; deleting this removes norm, identity, framing, and micro-foundations.

## Mastery gate

Interaction diagnosis + influence boundary + observation design

## Formation modes

R, S, P, M, D, A, B, F, V, L
