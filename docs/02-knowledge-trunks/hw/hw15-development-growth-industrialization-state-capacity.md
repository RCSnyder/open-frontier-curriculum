# HW15  -  Development, Growth, Industrialization & State Capacity

**Branch:** Human Worlds
**Status:** candidate-v2
**Prerequisites:** HW08, HW09, HW10, HW11, HW12, HW13, HW14

## Exit capability

Compare mechanisms of long-run development and explain why similar policies diverge across institutional contexts.

## Core concepts

productivity, structural transformation, industrialization, institutions, human capital, infrastructure, welfare, poverty, state capacity

## Deletion test

Frontier technology lives inside development trajectories; growth and state capacity are not just firm-level economics.

## Mastery gate
