# HW17  -  Conflict, War, Empire, Diplomacy & International Order

**Branch:** Human Worlds
**Status:** candidate-v2
**Prerequisites:** HW03, HW07, HW10, HW14, HW16

## Exit capability

Model conflict and bargaining under coercion, uncertainty, identity, and institutional constraints.

## Core concepts

security dilemma, deterrence, alliances, war, civil conflict, empire, colonialism, diplomacy, sovereignty, international order

## Deletion test

Large-scale coercion and geopolitical order cannot be safely treated as a special case of domestic politics.

## Mastery gate

Crisis bargaining + escalation ladder + historical comparison

## Formation modes

R, S, P, M, D, A, B, F, V, L

## AI modes
