# EX-M09  -  Poetry: Rhythm, Meter, Line & Sound

**Branch:** Expression & Interpretation
**Status:** candidate-strong
**Prerequisites:** EX-M01, EX-M02, EX-M03, EX-M04, EX-M05, EX-M06

## Exit capability

Hear, scan, and deliberately organize sound and line so form contributes to meaning rather than decorating it.

## Core concepts

stress, rhythm, foot, meter, iamb, cadence, rhyme, alliteration, assonance, consonance, line, enjambment, caesura, stanza, free verse

## Deletion test

If missing, a major expressive dimension of English - timing, recurrence, sonic expectation, and lineation - remains uncontrolled.

## Mastery gate

Scansion + line-break transformation + compose under form constraint

## Formation modes

R, S, P, M, D, B, C, V, L

## AI modes

A0, A1, A2, A4, A5

## Nodes / outcomes

| ID | Capability | Mastery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EX-LO-033 | Scan common accentual-syllabic lines and distinguish meter from actual performed rhythm. | Reconstruct |
| EX-LO-034 | Use rhyme, stress, alliteration, assonance, consonance, caesura, and repetition deliberately. | Transfer |
| EX-LO-035 | Explain how line breaks and enjambment alter syntax, pace, emphasis, and ambiguity. | Break / Bound |
| EX-LO-036 | Compose one idea in metrical verse, free verse, and rhythmic prose, then defend the formal tradeoffs. | Check / Defend |
