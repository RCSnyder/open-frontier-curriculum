# EX-M11  -  Close Reading, Form, Genre & Literary Tradition

**Branch:** Expression & Interpretation
**Status:** candidate-strong
**Prerequisites:** EX-M01, EX-M02, EX-M03, EX-M04, EX-M05, EX-M06, EX-M07, EX-M08, EX-M09, EX-M10

## Exit capability

Read unfamiliar literary language closely, identify formal choices, situate them historically, and defend interpretations without pretending ambiguity is arbitrary.

## Core concepts

form/content, genre, convention, motif, ambiguity, irony, allusion, intertextuality, historical language change, context, multiple interpretation

## Deletion test

If missing, literature becomes either plot-summary or taste; the learner cannot reverse-engineer expressive possibility from masterworks.

## Mastery gate

Unseen close reading + competing interpretation + historical boundary

## Formation modes

R, S, P, M, D, B, C, V, L

## AI modes

A0, A1, A2, A4, A5

## Nodes / outcomes

| ID | Capability | Mastery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EX-LO-041 | Perform an unseen close reading at the levels of syntax, diction, image, sound, form, voice, and structure. | Reconstruct |
| EX-LO-042 | Distinguish genre convention from an author's specific deviation from convention. | Transfer |
| EX-LO-043 | Place major English-language forms and periods on a rough historical map without treating the map as a hierarchy of value. | Break / Bound |
| EX-LO-044 | Defend two competing interpretations of one passage and identify which details discriminate between them. | Check / Defend |
