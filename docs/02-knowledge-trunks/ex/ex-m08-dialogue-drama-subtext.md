# EX-M08  -  Dialogue, Drama & Subtext

**Branch:** Expression & Interpretation
**Status:** candidate-strong
**Prerequisites:** EX-M01, EX-M02, EX-M03, EX-M04, EX-M05, EX-M06, EX-M07

## Exit capability

Write speech that simultaneously performs action, reveals character, withholds information, and advances conflict.

## Core concepts

speech act, turn-taking, subtext, dramatic objective, conflict, interruption, silence, monologue, soliloquy, scene beat, stage information

## Deletion test

If missing, represented conversation becomes exposition with quotation marks rather than social action.

## Mastery gate

Subtext scene + speech-act analysis + performance defense

## Formation modes

R, S, P, M, D, B, C, V, L

## AI modes

A0, A1, A2, A4, A5

## Nodes / outcomes

| ID | Capability | Mastery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EX-LO-029 | Identify speech acts and objectives beneath dialogue lines rather than treating dialogue as information transfer only. | Reconstruct |
| EX-LO-030 | Write a two-person scene where each speaker pursues a different objective and important information remains implicit. | Transfer |
| EX-LO-031 | Use interruption, silence, rhythm, gesture/stage information, and turn length as meaning-bearing devices. | Break / Bound |
| EX-LO-032 | Transform explanatory prose into playable dramatic action without losing necessary content. | Check / Defend |
