# EX-M06  -  Voice, Tone, Register & Persona

**Branch:** Expression & Interpretation
**Status:** candidate-strong
**Prerequisites:** EX-M01, EX-M02, EX-M03, EX-M04, EX-M05

## Exit capability

Produce recognizably different voices and registers without changing underlying facts; infer stance from linguistic evidence.

## Core concepts

speaker, implied author, persona, tone, stance, distance, formality, dialect, sociolect, code-switching, irony, reliability

## Deletion test

If missing, the writer cannot control social meaning, attitude, intimacy, authority, irony, or identity through language.

## Mastery gate

Same-content multi-register composition + tone defense

## Formation modes

R, S, P, M, D, B, C, V, L

## AI modes

A0, A1, A2, A4, A5

## Nodes / outcomes

| ID | Capability | Mastery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EX-LO-021 | Describe voice and tone through observable lexical, syntactic, rhythmic, and rhetorical features. | Reconstruct |
| EX-LO-022 | Rewrite one factual content block in at least five registers while keeping core facts invariant. | Transfer |
| EX-LO-023 | Construct reliable and unreliable personae and show how evidence creates the reader's trust or suspicion. | Break / Bound |
| EX-LO-024 | Use irony and distance without making the intended stance unrecoverable. | Check / Defend |

## Entry sources

- **Model fiction**  -  [Pride and Prejudice](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/1342)
- **Model essay**  -  [Politics and the English Language](https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/politics-and-the-english-language/)
