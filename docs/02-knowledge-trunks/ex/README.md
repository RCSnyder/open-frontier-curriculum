# Expression & Interpretation

Grammar, diction, rhetoric, argument, narrative, literature, interpretation, speech, style and revision.

## Why this trunk survives deletion

Thought cannot be reliably externalized, transformed for audience and medium, interpreted from difficult works or defended in language.

## Modules

| ID | Module | Exit capability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EX-M01 | Grammar & Sentence Architecture | Build, parse, combine, split, and deliberately vary English sentences while preserving exact logical relations. |
| EX-M02 | Diction, Semantics, Idiom & Lexical Precision | Choose words for exact meaning, implication, register, rhythm, and social effect; diagnose near-synonym differences. |
| EX-M03 | Cohesion, Paragraphs & Information Flow | Control what the reader knows, expects, and notices from sentence to sentence and paragraph to paragraph. |
| EX-M04 | Rhetoric, Argument, Audience & Occasion | Make a precise case for a skeptical audience, adapt it to occasion, and distinguish persuasion from proof. |
| EX-M05 | Image, Figurative Language & Analogy | Render perception, emotion, abstraction, and relation through concrete language and controlled figurative structures. |
| EX-M06 | Voice, Tone, Register & Persona | Produce recognizably different voices and registers without changing underlying facts; infer stance from linguistic evidence. |
| EX-M07 | Narrative: Point of View, Time, Scene & Summary | Narrate the same events under different viewpoints, time structures, and information constraints while controlling reader inference. |
| EX-M08 | Dialogue, Drama & Subtext | Write speech that simultaneously performs action, reveals character, withholds information, and advances conflict. |
| EX-M09 | Poetry: Rhythm, Meter, Line & Sound | Hear, scan, and deliberately organize sound and line so form contributes to meaning rather than decorating it. |
| EX-M10 | Essay, Exposition, Definition & Criticism | Explain difficult ideas clearly, define contested terms, compare models, and write interpretive criticism supported by textual evidence. |
| EX-M11 | Close Reading, Form, Genre & Literary Tradition | Read unfamiliar literary language closely, identify formal choices, situate them historically, and defend interpretations without pretending ambiguity is arbitrary. |
| EX-M12 | Imitation, Transformation & Register Translation | Preserve a chosen semantic core while radically altering voice, form, length, audience, or genre; imitate mechanisms without copying wording. |
| EX-M13 | Oral English, Cadence, Speech & Performance | Turn written English into intelligible, persuasive, memorable speech and compose with the ear as well as the eye. |
| EX-M14 | Integrated Expressive Command & Editorial Judgment | Express the same difficult content as explanation, argument, narrative, dialogue, lyric, speech, and concise summary; edit each toward exact intended effect. |
