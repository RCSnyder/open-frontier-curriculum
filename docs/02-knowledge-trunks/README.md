# Six knowledge trunks

| ID | Trunk | Status | Deletion failure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TS | Technical Systems | live-foundation | The learner cannot independently model, measure, build, operate or audit physical and technical systems. |
| PH | Philosophy & Judgment | candidate-retained | The learner can execute but cannot reliably distinguish conceptual, empirical, logical and normative disputes or justify beliefs and values. |
| HW | Human Worlds | candidate-v2 | The learner treats people and institutions as noise around technical systems and cannot reason about incentives, power, coordination or institutional change. |
| EX | Expression & Interpretation | candidate-strong | Thought cannot be reliably externalized, transformed for audience and medium, interpreted from difficult works or defended in language. |
| ML | Mind, Learning & Agency | candidate | The architecture lacks an operational theory of how humans acquire, retain, transfer, regulate and renew competence. |
| DR | Design & Representation | candidate-compression-test | Visual-spatial, interface and aesthetic-functional failures recur even when technical and verbal competence remain intact. |
