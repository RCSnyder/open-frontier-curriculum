# Expressive Command

**Representation**

## How can I interpret, articulate and give form to understanding?

Read and listen closely, express ideas with precision, and make language, images, space and material form answerable to meaning and use.

## Starting points

Choose an entry that fits your preparation. This is not a mandatory sequence.

- **[Reading literature as a whole work](../learn/reading-literature.md)**. Study and practice.
- **[Rhetoric, Argument, Audience & Occasion](../learn/ex-m04.md)**. Study outline.
- **[Interaction, Interface & Service Design](../learn/dr08.md)**. Study and practice.

Language and literary interpretation sit alongside visual, spatial and material representation. Their methods differ; their shared concern is meaningful form.
