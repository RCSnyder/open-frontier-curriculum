# Human-systems reference map

Sources are labeled by **epistemic role** so that memoir, normative argument, formal theory, empirical scholarship, and practitioner frameworks are not treated as interchangeable evidence.

## Source criticism

**A People's History of the United States: Howard Zinn**  
`Counter-history / polemical synthesis` | Wave 4 W125

**Use:** Triangulate a contested claim against primary sources and competing historians.

**Caution:** Do not treat as neutral consensus; disagreement is allowed and expected.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Political concepts

**The Blackwell Encyclopaedia of Political Thought: David Miller et al. (eds.)**  
`Reference work` | Wave 4 W127

**Use:** Define authority, liberty, justice, equality, democracy, rights and legitimacy before using them.

**Caution:** Reference has plural traditions, not one unified doctrine.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Race/objectives

**How to Be an Antiracist: Ibram X. Kendi**  
`Normative argument` | Wave 4 W126

**Use:** Translate normative claims into explicit objective functions, mechanisms and empirical questions.

**Caution:** Normative premise ≠ empirical causal proof.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Sociotechnical design

**Design Justice: Sasha Costanza-Chock**  
`Participatory design framework / scholarship` | Wave 4 W126

**Use:** Ask who defines the problem, participates, benefits, bears harms and can appeal.

**Caution:** Pair participation claims with actual domain evidence.

**Access:** [Full open text](https://designjustice.mitpress.mit.edu/)

## Technology/race

**Race After Technology: Ruha Benjamin**  
`Critical sociotechnical analysis` | Wave 4 W126 or track eval

**Use:** Look for mechanisms that reproduce hierarchy without explicit protected-class variables.

**Caution:** Pair with quantitative subgroup/causal analysis.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Ranking/discrimination

**Algorithms of Oppression: Safiya Umoja Noble**  
`Critical case analysis` | AI/Platform track + W126/W128

**Use:** Map ranking objective, labels, commercial incentives and exposure harms.

**Caution:** Historical cases should not be assumed unchanged in current systems.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Political incentives

**The Logic of Political Survival: Bueno de Mesquita, Smith, Siverson, Morrow**  
`Formal political theory` | Wave 4 W127

**Use:** Map selectorate/coalition incentives and public/private goods in deployment governance.

**Caution:** Strong model with boundary conditions, not a universal law.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Commons

**Governing the Commons: Elinor Ostrom**  
`Empirical institutional theory` | Wave 4 W127/W135

**Use:** Design governance for shared resources beyond state-vs-market binary.

**Caution:** Local conditions matter; avoid turning design principles into formulas.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Exit/voice

**Exit, Voice, and Loyalty: Albert O. Hirschman**  
`Institutional model` | Wave 4 W127/W135

**Use:** Design meaningful exit, complaint, appeal and repair channels.

**Caution:** Exit can be impossible or costly; map power and switching costs.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Legibility

**Seeing Like a State: James C. Scott**  
`Comparative historical argument` | W127/W130

**Use:** List what an optimization/model makes legible and what local knowledge it destroys.

**Caution:** Pair critique with cases where standardization is beneficial.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Strategic interaction

**The Strategy of Conflict: Thomas C. Schelling**  
`Game theory / strategic theory` | W127/W131

**Use:** Analyze commitment, bargaining, focal points, threats and coordination.

**Caution:** Formal strategic intuition needs empirical/institutional context.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Collective action

**The Logic of Collective Action: Mancur Olson**  
`Political economy theory` | W127/W135

**Use:** Predict free-riding and concentrated-vs-diffuse incentives.

**Caution:** Compare with Ostrom and real institutions.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Attention/platforms

**Ten Arguments for Deleting Your Social Media Accounts Right Now: Jaron Lanier**  
`Polemical technology critique` | Wave 4 W128

**Use:** Convert rhetoric into measurable attention/incentive hypotheses.

**Caution:** Effect sizes require empirical research beyond the book.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Platform organization

**Careless People: Sarah Wynn-Williams**  
`Memoir / insider narrative` | Wave 4 W128

**Use:** Separate witnessed event, interpretation, hearsay, incentives and corroboration.

**Caution:** Memoir is situated evidence, not total causal history.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Violence/bystanders

**The Roots of Evil: Ervin Staub**  
`Social-psychological theory` | Wave 4 W129

**Use:** Causal-map difficult conditions, identity, ideology, bystanders and escalating harm.

**Caution:** Pair with comparative history and newer empirical work.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Prevention/reconciliation

**Overcoming Evil: Ervin Staub**  
`Applied social psychology` | Wave 4 W129

**Use:** Turn prevention claims into intervention mechanisms and measurable outcomes.

**Caution:** Separate moral goal from intervention efficacy.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Normalization of deviance

**The Challenger Launch Decision: Diane Vaughan**  
`Organizational sociology / case study` | Wave 4 W129

**Use:** Model how normal routines can normalize anomalous risk without malicious actors.

**Caution:** Do not reduce every failure to 'normalization of deviance'.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## System safety

**Engineering a Safer World: Nancy Leveson**  
`Systems safety theory/practice` | Wave 2 + W129/W133

**Use:** Model unsafe control, constraints, interaction and organization.

**Caution:** Open, technical anchor against simplistic root-cause narratives.

**Access:** [Open access](https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262533690/engineering-a-safer-world/)

## Complex-system accidents

**Normal Accidents: Charles Perrow**  
`Sociological systems-risk theory` | W129 / safety track

**Use:** Ask when tight coupling and interactive complexity make accident propagation hard to anticipate.

**Caution:** Compare with Leveson; not every complex system is doomed.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Wicked problems

**Dilemmas in a General Theory of Planning: Horst Rittel, Melvin Webber**  
`Planning theory` | Wave 4 W130

**Use:** Identify contested formulations, no definitive stopping rule and one-shot consequences.

**Caution:** Do not use 'wicked' as excuse to abandon quantitative analysis.

**Access:** [Legal public PDF](https://www.cc.gatech.edu/fac/ellendo/rittel/rittel-dilemma.pdf)

## Communication

**Nonviolent Communication: Marshall Rosenberg / CNVC**  
`Practitioner framework` | Wave 4 W131

**Use:** Separate observation, feeling/need and request; test whether it reduces avoidable escalation.

**Caution:** Useful practice framework, not settled universal psychology.

**Access:** [Free framework page; book proprietary](https://www.cnvc.org/learn/what-is-nvc)

## Negotiation

**Difficult Conversations: Douglas Stone, Bruce Patton, Sheila Heen**  
`Negotiation / practitioner synthesis` | Wave 4 W131

**Use:** Separate What Happened, Feelings and Identity conversations; move toward learning.

**Caution:** Not a substitute for structural power analysis.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Team practice

**The 15 Commitments of Conscious Leadership: Jim Dethmer, Diana Chapman, Kaley Klemp**  
`Practitioner framework` | Wave 4 W131/W135

**Use:** Experiment with responsibility, curiosity and candid feedback in team retrospectives.

**Caution:** Require evidence of improved learning/accountability; do not treat as science.

**Access:** [Free practitioner resources](https://conscious.is/resources/)

## Scientific change

**The Structure of Scientific Revolutions: Thomas Kuhn**  
`History/philosophy of science` | W125/W134

**Use:** Distinguish normal science, anomalies, paradigm language and retrospective narratives.

**Caution:** Do not use 'paradigm shift' as prestige language for every novelty.

**Access:** Library / proprietary

## Forecasting/judgment

**Superforecasting: Philip Tetlock, Dan Gardner**  
`Empirical/practitioner synthesis` | Throughout; W132/W136

**Use:** Use explicit probabilities, decomposition, updating and scoring for uncertain forecasts.

**Caution:** Forecast accuracy is not equivalent to moral or causal judgment.

**Access:** Library / proprietary
