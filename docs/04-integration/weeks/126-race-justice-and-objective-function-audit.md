# Week 126: Race, justice and objective-function audit

**Theme:** Objectives

**Reading/source:** Ibram X. Kendi, How to Be an Antiracist (selected argument) + Sasha Costanza-Chock, Design Justice (open) + empirical subgroup evidence relevant to your domain

**Learning outcome:** Translate normative claims into explicit objectives, mechanisms, distributions of benefit/harm and testable questions.

**Required analysis:** Audit your capstone objective: who defines success, whose errors count, subgroup failure, recourse and distributional effects.

**Artifact that must change:** Add a distributional/recourse section to your system specification or evaluation plan.

**Oral defense:** Can two reasonable moral objectives imply different engineering designs even with identical facts?

**Pass criterion:** Pass if the technical objective/evaluation changes; agreement with any author is not required.

**Function:** Objective design / inclusion

**Access/source:** https://designjustice.mitpress.mit.edu/
