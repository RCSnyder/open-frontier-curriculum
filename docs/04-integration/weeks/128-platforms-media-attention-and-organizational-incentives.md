# Week 128: Platforms, media, attention and organizational incentives

**Theme:** Attention

**Reading/source:** Jaron Lanier, Ten Arguments... + Sarah Wynn-Williams, Careless People + empirical research on your platform/attention hypothesis

**Learning outcome:** Distinguish memoir/polemic from empirical evidence; map product metrics to organizational incentives and externalities.

**Required analysis:** Turn three claims from the readings into measurable hypotheses; seek confirming and disconfirming evidence.

**Artifact that must change:** If your capstone has users/engagement/agents, add anti-Goodhart metrics and attention/manipulation abuse tests.

**Oral defense:** What behavior would your business/model metric reward that your stated mission condemns?

**Pass criterion:** Pass if rhetoric is converted into falsifiable claims and at least one hypothesis is weakened or revised.

**Function:** Media / organizational incentives

**Access/source:** Proprietary books; do not treat memoir as complete causal record.
