# Week 127: Political thought, incentives and institutional control

**Theme:** Power

**Reading/source:** Blackwell Encyclopaedia of Political Thought (reference) + The Logic of Political Survival + Ostrom, Governing the Commons + Hirschman, Exit, Voice, and Loyalty

**Learning outcome:** Model authority, selector/coalition incentives, commons, exit, voice, ownership and institutional failure.

**Required analysis:** Create power map: decision rights, vetoes, funding, ownership, users, affected non-users, regulators, operators, maintainers and appeal routes.

**Artifact that must change:** Redesign one governance mechanism for your capstone based on the power map.

**Oral defense:** Who can make the system continue operating after the evidence says stop?

**Pass criterion:** Pass if at least one incentive conflict produces a changed control/ownership/oversight design.

**Function:** Political economy / institutions

**Access/source:** Books proprietary; source via library.
