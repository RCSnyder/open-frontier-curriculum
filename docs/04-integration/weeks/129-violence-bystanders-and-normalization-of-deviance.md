# Week 129: Violence, bystanders and normalization of deviance

**Theme:** Failure

**Reading/source:** Ervin Staub, The Roots of Evil / Overcoming Evil + Diane Vaughan, The Challenger Launch Decision + Nancy Leveson

**Learning outcome:** Understand escalation, bystander action, normalization, organizational drift and system-level accident causation.

**Required analysis:** Reconstruct a real incident as an escalation/control-structure timeline rather than a single-root-cause story.

**Artifact that must change:** Add leading indicators, dissent/stop-work channels and drift monitoring to capstone operations.

**Oral defense:** What weak signal will your organization normalize after it has succeeded ten times?

**Pass criterion:** Pass if incident analysis changes monitoring, authority or shutdown design.

**Function:** Moral psychology / organizational safety

**Access/source:** Leveson open; others library.
