# Week 125: History and source criticism

**Theme:** Epistemics

**Reading/source:** Howard Zinn, A People's History (selected chapter) + primary sources + a competing mainstream historical account

**Learning outcome:** Separate event, source, selection, framing and inference; detect omitted actors and asymmetric evidence.

**Required analysis:** Build a claim ledger for one consequential technology/history claim: exact claim, primary evidence, competing interpretation, uncertainty.

**Artifact that must change:** Rewrite one paragraph of your capstone history/context section after source triangulation.

**Oral defense:** Why did you select these sources, and what source would most threaten your preferred narrative?

**Pass criterion:** Pass if the final narrative contains at least one material revision caused by contrary evidence.

**Function:** Source criticism / historical judgment

**Access/source:** Proprietary book; use library. Pairing is mandatory.
