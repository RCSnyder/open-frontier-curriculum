# Week 130: Wicked problems and limits of optimizer metaphors

**Theme:** Wickedness

**Reading/source:** Rittel & Webber, Dilemmas in a General Theory of Planning + selected wicked-problem literature

**Learning outcome:** Recognize problems with contested formulations, plural values, one-shot consequences and changing requirements.

**Required analysis:** Write three incompatible formulations of the same frontier deployment and show how each changes the optimum.

**Artifact that must change:** Replace a single optimum with reversible portfolio, monitoring triggers, recourse and reformulation schedule.

**Oral defense:** What if solving your formulation makes the underlying social problem worse?

**Pass criterion:** Pass if learner explicitly identifies where quantitative optimization remains valid and where it does not decide the objective.

**Function:** Problem formulation / governance

**Access/source:** https://www.cc.gatech.edu/fac/ellendo/rittel/rittel-dilemma.pdf
