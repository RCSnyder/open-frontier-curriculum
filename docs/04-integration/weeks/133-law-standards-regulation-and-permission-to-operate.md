# Week 133: Law, standards, regulation and permission to operate

**Theme:** Rules

**Reading/source:** Domain-specific primary regulation/standards + safety/security requirements

**Learning outcome:** Distinguish legal minimum, industry standard, safety case, ethical commitment and internal policy.

**Required analysis:** Build a compliance/standards matrix for capstone: requirement, authority, evidence, owner, verification and update trigger.

**Artifact that must change:** Identify one design decision that becomes different because of a standard/regulatory obligation.

**Oral defense:** Where is compliance necessary but insufficient for safety or legitimacy?

**Pass criterion:** Pass if primary authority is cited and requirements are traceable to verification evidence.

**Function:** Regulation / standards

**Access/source:** Use current primary/official sources for chosen domain.
