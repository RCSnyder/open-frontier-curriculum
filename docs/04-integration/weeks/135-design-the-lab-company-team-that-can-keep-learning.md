# Week 135: Design the lab/company/team that can keep learning

**Theme:** Institution

**Reading/source:** Ostrom/Hirschman/Leveson/Edmondson-style organizational learning + your own operating doctrine

**Learning outcome:** Design decision rights, dissent, review, incentives, documentation, hiring and knowledge retention.

**Required analysis:** Write a 2-page lab/company constitution: mission, non-goals, decision rights, stop-work authority, review cadence, evidence standards, conflict process.

**Artifact that must change:** Run a tabletop scenario: founder/PI wants to ship; safety lead objects; funder deadline looms; data are ambiguous.

**Oral defense:** What organizational rule remains good when you personally are no longer in charge?

**Pass criterion:** Pass if structure protects dissent and evidence against both bureaucracy and charismatic override.

**Function:** Institution design

**Access/source:** Synthesis from prior sources; explicitly marked as design proposal.
