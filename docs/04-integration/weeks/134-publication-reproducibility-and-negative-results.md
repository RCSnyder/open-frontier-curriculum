# Week 134: Publication, reproducibility and negative results

**Theme:** Science

**Reading/source:** Your track's primary paper + reproducibility/open-science guidance

**Learning outcome:** Write claims with evidence tiers; package code/data/configuration; report null/negative/adversarial results.

**Required analysis:** Reproduce your capstone from clean environment; another person follows README without oral help.

**Artifact that must change:** Create release package: paper, data provenance, environment, tests, limitations, negative-results appendix.

**Oral defense:** What claim in your abstract is least supported by the artifacts?

**Pass criterion:** Pass if an independent person can reproduce the central figure/result and identify unsupported claims.

**Function:** Research integrity / publication

**Access/source:** Use repository/archive appropriate to field.
