# L03  -  Relationships, Family & Care

Build trustworthy relationships; communicate needs; establish consent/boundaries; repair conflict; nurture family/community; care for dependents.

## Why it matters

A technically brilliant but relationally incapable person is not broadly competent in life.

## Competencies

| ID | Competency | Knowledge | Judgment | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L03-C01 | Listening & perspective taking | active listening; empathy; inference vs observation; cultural context | Represent another person's position fairly before responding | Structured listening interview | Partner-rated summary accuracy |
| L03-C02 | Friendship & trust | reciprocity; reliability; disclosure; boundaries; repair | Distinguish intimacy from dependency, loyalty from enabling, trust from naivety | Maintain/repair a real commitment | Reflection + counterpart feedback |
| L03-C03 | Consent, boundaries & autonomy | consent; coercion; privacy; power; refusal; negotiation | Recognize power asymmetry and preserve the other's meaningful ability to say no | Role-play boundary/consent negotiations | Observed scenario + redo |
| L03-C04 | Conflict, negotiation & repair | interests; positions; emotion; status; apology; mediation; bargaining | Separate factual, value, identity, and relationship conflict; choose process accordingly | Mediate or resolve a structured conflict | Agreement/repair record + postmortem |
| L03-C05 | Family systems & caregiving | family roles; development; dependency; care work; kinship; intergenerational obligations | Balance autonomy, duty, limited resources, and diverse family forms without one idealized template | Complete a real care/service responsibility | Care log + reflection + recipient feedback where appropriate |
| L03-C06 | Belonging, hospitality & community | social networks; inclusion; loneliness; ritual; community norms | Create connection without coercive conformity; notice exclusion and power | Host/organize a recurring small community activity | Participation + learning review |
