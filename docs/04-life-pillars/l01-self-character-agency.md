# L01  -  Self, Character & Agency

Direct one's attention, commitments, habits, decisions, identity, and use of time.

## Why it matters

A person who cannot govern themselves cannot reliably deploy any other competence.

## Competencies

| ID | Competency | Knowledge | Judgment | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L01-C01 | Values, commitments & goal hierarchy | values; goals; identity; opportunity cost; roles | Distinguish chosen commitments from inherited/status incentives; resolve conflicts among goals | Write and revise a one-year / five-year goal hierarchy | Goal map + decision postmortem |
| L01-C02 | Attention & time governance | attention; calendars; deep work; interruption; energy rhythms | Know what deserves attention and what should be refused/deferred | Run a 3-week attention/time experiment | Time audit + before/after evidence |
| L01-C03 | Habits & environment design | cue; friction; routine; reinforcement; precommitment | Prefer system design to heroic willpower when behavior is predictable | Build one useful habit and remove one harmful loop | Habit protocol + adherence data |
| L01-C04 | Emotion & self-regulation | emotion; appraisal; stress; impulse; recovery | Recognize when emotion is information, noise, or a state requiring delay/help | Use a regulation protocol in a real stressful decision | Reflection + observed/recorded outcome |
| L01-C05 | Decision process & calibration | base rates; forecasts; expected value; uncertainty; regret; reversibility | Match decision process to stakes, uncertainty, and reversibility | Maintain decision/forecast journal | Calibration and decision review |
| L01-C06 | Meaning, identity & mortality | identity; narrative; vocation; suffering; mortality; philosophical/religious worldviews | Hold existential commitments reflectively without confusing meaning with empirical proof | Write and defend a personal philosophy provisionally | Essay + dialogue + revision |
