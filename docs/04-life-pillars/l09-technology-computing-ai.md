# L09  -  Technology, Computing & AI

Understand computational systems, digital infrastructure, privacy/security, AI capabilities/limits, delegation, evaluation, and governance.

## Why it matters

AI-rich civilization makes technical dependence a civic and personal issue.

## Competencies

| ID | Competency | Knowledge | Judgment | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L09-C01 | Computational thinking & algorithms | abstraction; decomposition; algorithm; complexity; data structures; automation | Know when a computational formulation helps and where verification costs dominate | Implement and compare simple algorithms | Code + complexity/assumption defense |
| L09-C02 | Software, data & network literacy | software architecture; databases; APIs; networks; distributed failure; observability | Recognize that 'the app works' is not the same as secure/reliable infrastructure | Build a small networked system and break it | System diagram + incident exercise |
| L09-C03 | Cybersecurity, privacy & identity | threat models; authentication; phishing; backups; encryption concepts; permissions; privacy | Match protection to threat, preserve recovery, minimize unnecessary data exposure | Harden personal digital setup | Threat model + recovery drill |
| L09-C04 | AI mental models & capability limits | training/inference; representations; context; tools; agents; evals; uncertainty | Reason from evidence about a system rather than anthropomorphic intuition | Compare models on a controlled task suite | Eval report + limitation statement |
| L09-C05 | AI delegation, provenance & verification | task contracts; permissions; provenance; evals; monitoring; rollback | Delegate execution without delegating accountability blindly | Run an agentic project with hidden tests | Audit log + human defense |
| L09-C06 | Digital governance, rights & concentration | platform power; standards; surveillance; speech; competition; infrastructure; AI governance | Recognize technical choices as institutional choices when they structure others' agency | Analyze a platform/model governance case | Governance map + reform options |
