# L06  -  Work, Craft, Enterprise & Service

Develop valuable skill, manage projects, collaborate, operate systems, understand organizations/enterprise, and serve real users or communities.

## Why it matters

Work is where knowledge meets responsibility, production, incentives, and other people.

## Competencies

| ID | Competency | Knowledge | Judgment | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L06-C01 | Craft apprenticeship & standards | skill decomposition; standards; tacit knowledge; deliberate practice; quality | Know what 'good work' looks like and how to learn from expert critique | Pursue one craft/technical skill for 100+ deliberate hours | Before/after portfolio + external review |
| L06-C02 | Project formulation & execution | scope; requirements; milestones; dependencies; risk; change control | Protect the objective while changing the plan when evidence changes | Run a 12–24 week project | Project log + delivered artifact |
| L06-C03 | Teamwork, leadership & followership | roles; trust; coordination; feedback; accountability; leadership; service | Lead when needed, follow competently, and make dissent safe | Rotate roles on a real team | 360 feedback + incident review |
| L06-C04 | Operations, quality & reliability | process; queue; bottleneck; standard work; quality; maintenance; incident learning | Improve throughput without hiding defects or externalizing risk | Map and improve a recurring process | Before/after process metrics |
| L06-C05 | Enterprise, customers & value creation | need discovery; product/service; pricing; unit economics; competition; ethics | Distinguish actual value from persuasion and extractive lock-in | Serve a real customer/user or run micro-enterprise simulation | User evidence + unit economics |
| L06-C06 | Career, reputation & service | portfolio; signaling; networks; negotiation; professional ethics; contribution | Choose work using capability, values, constraints, learning, and service - not prestige alone | Build portfolio/CV, conduct informational interviews, contribute publicly | External review + opportunity decision memo |
