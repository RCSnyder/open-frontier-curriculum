# L12  -  Nature, Place, Risk & Future Stewardship

Understand Earth systems, ecology, energy, food/water/material flows, cities/place, hazards, resilience, long-term futures, and intergenerational responsibility.

## Why it matters

Technological civilization remains embedded in planetary and temporal constraints.

## Competencies

| ID | Competency | Knowledge | Judgment | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L12-C01 | Earth systems & ecology | climate; water; soils; biogeochemical cycles; ecosystems; biodiversity; feedbacks | Distinguish local observation, model projection, uncertainty, and value choices | Field/system map of a local ecosystem/resource | Map + measurements + source review |
| L12-C02 | Energy, materials & industrial metabolism | energy conversion; grids; fuels; mining; materials; waste; lifecycle | Compare systems using physical constraints, costs, externalities, and scale | Trace an everyday product/energy service from resource to waste | Lifecycle/energy flow model |
| L12-C03 | Food, agriculture & water systems | agronomy; soil; irrigation; fisheries; supply chains; nutrition; land use | Reason across yield, resilience, ecology, labor, culture, and access | Trace a food/water system | System map + intervention critique |
| L12-C04 | Place, cities & built environment | land use; housing; transport; infrastructure; public space; zoning; urban form | Understand how physical form encodes incentives, access, health, and social life | Conduct a neighborhood/place audit | Map + observation + redesign |
| L12-C05 | Hazards, resilience & continuity | risk; disaster; redundancy; preparedness; supply chains; insurance; emergency governance | Prioritize catastrophic tails and graceful degradation without paranoia | Build household/team continuity plan and run a drill | Scenario performance + after-action report |
| L12-C06 | Futures & intergenerational stewardship | forecasting; scenarios; technological change; option value; irreversibility; existential/global catastrophic risk | Separate prediction from scenario planning; preserve plural futures and rights under uncertainty | Write a 25-year decision under multiple futures | Scenario tree + reversible strategy + defense |
