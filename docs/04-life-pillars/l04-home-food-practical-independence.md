# L04  -  Home, Food & Practical Independence

Operate a household: food, cleaning, maintenance, tools, transport, logistics, safety, emergency readiness, and basic repair.

## Why it matters

Daily life contains physical systems and failure modes; dependence can become fragility.

## Competencies

| ID | Competency | Knowledge | Judgment | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L04-C01 | Food preparation & kitchen systems | knife/heat safety; recipes; timing; preservation; sanitation; pantry systems | Balance cost, nutrition, time, waste, preference, and safety | Cook multiple complete meals from raw ingredients | Live cooking + food-safety check |
| L04-C02 | Cleaning, laundry & household hygiene | materials; surfactants; sanitation; textiles; routines | Use enough cleanliness for health/material care without magical thinking or waste | Run a household maintenance schedule | Inspection + materials reasoning |
| L04-C03 | Tools, fasteners & basic repair | hand tools; power-tool basics; fasteners; adhesives; troubleshooting; tolerances | Know when to repair, replace, stop, or hire a qualified trade | Repair or assemble several real objects | Live tool demonstration + repair artifact |
| L04-C04 | Utilities, home systems & energy | electricity; water; HVAC; ventilation; breakers; leaks; energy use | Recognize dangerous conditions and avoid unsafe DIY beyond competence | Map a home's major systems and shutoffs | System map + emergency drill |
| L04-C05 | Transport, navigation & logistics | maps; routing; vehicle/transit basics; trip planning; maintenance; accessibility | Trade cost/time/risk/reliability/environmental impact | Plan and execute a multi-leg real journey | Route plan + contingency + execution |
| L04-C06 | Emergency readiness & first response | hazards; first aid basics; emergency communications; fire/weather preparedness; supplies | Prioritize life safety, call qualified responders, and act within training | Complete recognized first-aid/CPR training where available and run drills | Credential/equivalent + scenario |
