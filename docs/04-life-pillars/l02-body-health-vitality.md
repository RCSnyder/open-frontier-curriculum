# L02  -  Body, Health & Vitality

Understand and care for the human body across prevention, movement, nutrition, sleep, mental health, sexuality, illness, and care navigation.

## Why it matters

Health is a prerequisite for agency and a high-stakes domain of misinformation.

## Competencies

| ID | Competency | Knowledge | Judgment | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L02-C01 | Health information & care navigation | health literacy; evidence; screening; prevention; medical system; informed consent | Know when self-care is appropriate and when qualified professional help is needed | Compare sources/services and prepare for a clinical visit | Health-source audit + care-navigation scenario |
| L02-C02 | Sleep, recovery & circadian health | sleep architecture; circadian rhythm; fatigue; recovery | Trade short-term productivity against long-term cognition/health realistically | Run a sleep/recovery experiment | Diary + performance comparison |
| L02-C03 | Nutrition & food environment | energy balance; macro/micronutrients; dietary patterns; food safety; marketing | Evaluate diet claims by evidence, context, sustainability, and feasibility | Plan/cook a week of nutritious food within constraints | Meal plan + cost/nutrition audit + cooking demo |
| L02-C04 | Movement, strength & aerobic capacity | fitness; strength; mobility; endurance; training adaptation; injury prevention | Select sustainable activity based on goals, health status, enjoyment, and risk | Complete a progressive training block | Baseline/post measures + technique check |
| L02-C05 | Mental health & help-seeking | stress; mood; anxiety; addiction; coping; social support; crisis recognition | Distinguish ordinary distress from patterns that warrant support; reject stigma and pseudo-diagnosis | Build support/resources plan; practice supportive conversation | Scenario + resource map |
| L02-C06 | Sexual/reproductive health, consent & safety | reproduction; contraception; STI prevention; consent; boundaries; sexual development | Make informed, respectful, risk-aware decisions with explicit consent and care | Analyze scenarios and locate valid services/information | Scenario defense + information audit |
