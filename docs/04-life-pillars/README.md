# Twelve life pillars

Branches organize knowledge. **Life pillars organize lived competence.** They are cross-trunk routes, not extra academic departments.

| ID | Pillar | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| L01 | Self, Character & Agency | Direct one's attention, commitments, habits, decisions, identity, and use of time. |
| L02 | Body, Health & Vitality | Understand and care for the human body across prevention, movement, nutrition, sleep, mental health, sexuality, illness, and care navigation. |
| L03 | Relationships, Family & Care | Build trustworthy relationships; communicate needs; establish consent/boundaries; repair conflict; nurture family/community; care for dependents. |
| L04 | Home, Food & Practical Independence | Operate a household: food, cleaning, maintenance, tools, transport, logistics, safety, emergency readiness, and basic repair. |
| L05 | Money, Property & Financial Agency | Manage cash flow, savings, debt, credit, insurance, investing, taxes, contracts, fraud, and long-horizon financial choices. |
| L06 | Work, Craft, Enterprise & Service | Develop valuable skill, manage projects, collaborate, operate systems, understand organizations/enterprise, and serve real users or communities. |
| L07 | Communication, Language & Public Expression | Read, write, speak, listen, persuade, narrate, explain, debate, and communicate across cultures and media. |
| L08 | Knowledge, Learning & Research | Search, evaluate sources, build knowledge, study effectively, reason statistically/causally, synthesize fields, and teach. |
| L09 | Technology, Computing & AI | Understand computational systems, digital infrastructure, privacy/security, AI capabilities/limits, delegation, evaluation, and governance. |
| L10 | Civic, Legal & Institutional Agency | Understand states, law, rights, public finance, media, organizations, collective action, power, and participation. |
| L11 | Culture, History, Beauty & Meaning | Encounter world history, literature, arts, music, architecture, religions/worldviews, rituals, philosophy, and the search for meaning. |
| L12 | Nature, Place, Risk & Future Stewardship | Understand Earth systems, ecology, energy, food/water/material flows, cities/place, hazards, resilience, long-term futures, and intergenerational responsibility. |
