# L05  -  Money, Property & Financial Agency

Manage cash flow, savings, debt, credit, insurance, investing, taxes, contracts, fraud, and long-horizon financial choices.

## Why it matters

Financial decisions compound for decades and are saturated with incentives and information asymmetry.

## Competencies

| ID | Competency | Knowledge | Judgment | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L05-C01 | Cash flow, budgeting & tradeoffs | income; expenses; fixed/variable costs; cash flow; inflation; opportunity cost | Build budgets that reflect values and uncertainty rather than false precision | Maintain a real/simulated monthly budget | Reconciled cash-flow model |
| L05-C02 | Saving, compounding & liquidity | interest; compounding; emergency reserves; time horizon | Trade liquidity, return, risk, and optionality | Model savings paths under shocks | Scenario model + decision memo |
| L05-C03 | Credit, debt & borrowing | APR; amortization; credit reports; collateral; default; predatory terms | Evaluate total cost and downside, not monthly payment alone | Compare three borrowing offers | Cost table + contract red-flag audit |
| L05-C04 | Risk, insurance & protection | risk pooling; deductible; limits; exclusions; liability; expected loss | Insure catastrophic risks before trivial volatility; understand exclusions | Analyze auto/home/health/disability/life examples appropriate to stage | Coverage-gap memo |
| L05-C05 | Investing, diversification & retirement | risk/return; diversification; fees; taxes; index concepts; retirement accounts; behavioral traps | Match portfolio complexity to goals, horizon, capacity, and evidence | Build a simple policy statement and stress test | Investment policy + drawdown scenario |
| L05-C06 | Taxes, contracts, fraud & consumer defense | tax basics; withholding; contracts; warranties; identity theft; scams; dispute/recourse | Read before signing; recognize asymmetric incentives and high-pressure tactics | Complete a sample return/contract/scam investigation | Document audit + recourse plan |
