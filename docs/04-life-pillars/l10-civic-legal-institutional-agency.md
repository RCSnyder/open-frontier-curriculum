# L10  -  Civic, Legal & Institutional Agency

Understand states, law, rights, public finance, media, organizations, collective action, power, and participation.

## Why it matters

People live under institutions whether or not they understand them.

## Competencies

| ID | Competency | Knowledge | Judgment | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L10-C01 | Civics, law & rights | state; constitution; rights; courts; regulation; local government; legal process | Know which rule system applies, what rights/obligations exist, and where recourse lies | Trace a real issue through institutions | Authority/recourse map |
| L10-C02 | Public finance & collective provision | taxation; budgets; public goods; externalities; welfare; infrastructure; debt | Connect benefits and costs across groups and time | Analyze a public budget/policy | Incidence + tradeoff memo |
| L10-C03 | Political competition & media | elections; parties; coalitions; public opinion; media systems; propaganda; agenda setting | Separate popularity, legality, legitimacy, and truth | Analyze a live/past campaign or information event | Source/media map + causal account |
| L10-C04 | Organizations, power & institutional behavior | bureaucracy; professions; delegation; metrics; capture; corruption; legitimacy | Analyze how rules on paper differ from incentives and actual practice | Map a school/company/public agency | Power/incentive map + redesign |
| L10-C05 | Deliberation, negotiation & pluralism | disagreement; compromise; rights; procedure; minority protections; public reasoning | Reach decisions without pretending value conflict disappears | Run a structured public deliberation | Decision record + dissent statement |
| L10-C06 | Civic participation & service | petition; meeting; testimony; volunteering; association; organizing; watchdog functions | Choose proportionate, lawful, evidence-aware forms of participation | Complete a local civic/service project | Public artifact + impact/postmortem |
