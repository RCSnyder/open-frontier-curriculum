# L07  -  Communication, Language & Public Expression

Read, write, speak, listen, persuade, narrate, explain, debate, and communicate across cultures and media.

## Why it matters

Most human coordination occurs through representation and language.

## Competencies

| ID | Competency | Knowledge | Judgment | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L07-C01 | Close reading & interpretation | genre; context; inference; ambiguity; rhetoric; evidence | Distinguish what a text says from what the reader projects | Annotate and defend an interpretation of a difficult text | Close-reading oral defense |
| L07-C02 | Expository writing | thesis; structure; paragraph logic; evidence; revision; style | Prefer clarity and warranted precision over impressive vagueness | Write and substantially revise a researched essay | Draft history + final + defense |
| L07-C03 | Argument & rhetoric | claims; reasons; warrants; audience; ethos/pathos/logos; framing; fallacies | Persuade without hiding decisive evidence or exploiting vulnerability | Deliver opposing arguments and strongest rebuttal | Speech/debate + self-critique |
| L07-C04 | Narrative & imagination | story; scene; character; point of view; metaphor; pacing | Use narrative to reveal experience without confusing a compelling story for causal proof | Write/perform a narrative artifact | Workshop + revision |
| L07-C05 | Listening, conversation & interviewing | questions; turn-taking; silence; paraphrase; interviewing; conversational repair | Know when to inquire rather than assert | Conduct a long-form interview and produce fair synthesis | Interview record + participant check |
| L07-C06 | Multilingual / cross-cultural communication | language learning; translation; register; code-switching; pragmatics; cultural conventions | Treat translation as interpretation and avoid assuming one's norms are universal | Reach functional competence in another language where feasible; compare translations | Conversation + translation commentary |
