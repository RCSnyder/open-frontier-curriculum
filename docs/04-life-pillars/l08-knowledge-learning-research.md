# L08  -  Knowledge, Learning & Research

Search, evaluate sources, build knowledge, study effectively, reason statistically/causally, synthesize fields, and teach.

## Why it matters

The learner must be capable of renewing the curriculum inside themselves.

## Competencies

| ID | Competency | Knowledge | Judgment | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L08-C01 | Information search & source ecology | search strategies; bibliographies; databases; primary/secondary sources; provenance | Use authority contextually; trace claims upstream | Build a source map for a contested question | Search log + source genealogy |
| L08-C02 | Learning system design | baseline; retrieval; spacing; interleaving; feedback; retention | Choose study method based on target learning, not subjective fluency | Design a 6-week study program | Delayed no-AI retest |
| L08-C03 | Quantitative and causal reasoning | statistics; uncertainty; confounding; experiments; causal diagrams; base rates | Match causal claim to design strength and uncertainty | Audit a public quantitative claim | Reanalysis + causal critique |
| L08-C04 | Research design & inquiry | question formulation; operationalization; method choice; ethics; replication | Ask discriminating questions and know what evidence could change the conclusion | Design and execute a small research project | Preregistered/declared plan + report |
| L08-C05 | Synthesis & knowledge architecture | concept maps; literature synthesis; contradictions; models; compression | Compress without erasing disagreement or uncertainty | Create a teachable synthesis across 10+ sources | Synthesis map + adversarial review |
| L08-C06 | Teaching & mentorship | diagnosis; explanation; worked examples; practice; feedback; motivation | Teach the learner rather than perform expertise at them | Teach a novice and measure learning | Pre/post evidence + lesson revision |
