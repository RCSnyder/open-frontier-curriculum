# L11  -  Culture, History, Beauty & Meaning

Encounter world history, literature, arts, music, architecture, religions/worldviews, rituals, philosophy, and the search for meaning.

## Why it matters

Human inheritance is more than utility; culture shapes identity, values, memory, and imagination.

## Competencies

| ID | Competency | Knowledge | Judgment | Practice | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L11-C01 | World-historical orientation | major eras; states/empires; religions; trade; industrialization; colonialism; decolonization; globalization | Use periodization as a tool, not destiny; avoid presentism | Build a comparative world timeline | Timeline + causal themes |
| L11-C02 | Religions, philosophies & worldviews | major religious/philosophical traditions; ritual; metaphysics; ethics; community | Describe traditions in terms adherents could recognize while retaining critical inquiry | Compare primary texts/practices from several traditions | Comparative seminar + reflection |
| L11-C03 | Literature & human experience | epic; drama; poetry; novel; essay; oral traditions; global literatures | Read for form, historical voice, moral imagination, and ambiguity rather than plot summary | Sustained close reading across eras/regions | Reading portfolio + seminar |
| L11-C04 | Visual art, architecture, music & performance | form; medium; style; patronage; ritual; space; sound; performance | See how formal choices and context jointly shape meaning | Analyze works in person when possible | Field notes + comparative criticism |
| L11-C05 | Taste, canon, criticism & curation | criteria; lineage; originality; convention; exclusion; popular/high art; AI abundance | Form preferences reflectively and revise criteria after comparison | Curate a small exhibition/anthology/program | Curatorial statement + blind ranking |
| L11-C06 | Meaning, ritual, memory & cultural creation | collective memory; ritual; festivals; monuments; language; identity; heritage | Preserve inheritance without freezing culture; create without treating tradition as costume | Create/participate in a meaningful cultural artifact or oral-history project | Artifact + context/provenance defense |
