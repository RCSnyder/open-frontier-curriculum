# About the project

**Unfinished public preview.** [Publication status and evidence](evidence.md)
records teaching availability, source-review coverage and what has not been established.

## Four foundations, integrated agency

The Open Frontier Curriculum is a reference and study system for independent
understanding, judgment, expression and responsible action.
Open Frontier contributes capability; Human Worlds contributes context;
Philosophy contributes judgment; Expressive Command contributes representation.
Praxis brings them together.

These are complementary intellectual responsibilities, not disjoint academic departments.
Learning practices support all four. Development shapes suitable entry routes.
Life contexts connect study to responsibility. AI changes the conditions of the work,
but human agency remains its purpose.

## What is established

The collection preserves six underlying branch classifications, including learning
and design, without requiring learners to treat them as six equivalent foundations.
Existing identifiers and links remain available. Subject pages join outcomes,
prerequisites, source recommendations and available teaching into one study home.

The public architecture is an editorial design, not evidence that four foundations
are a uniquely optimal decomposition. Sources and prerequisite claims require review.
Study outlines are not finished courses; supplied exercises are not validated assessments.
The childhood and adolescent routes are examples, not a complete K-12 curriculum.

Imported All Souls prompt mappings do not establish independent hold-out validation,
institutional endorsement or observed learner performance.
Reading areas awaiting selection are separated from identifiable works in the library.
Neither group is a guarantee of bibliographic accuracy or suitability for every learner.
