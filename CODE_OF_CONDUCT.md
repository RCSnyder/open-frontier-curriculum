# Code of conduct

Contributors are expected to make technical disagreement productive.

- Attack claims, assumptions, evidence, models, and designs: not people.
- State uncertainty and conflicts of interest when material.
- Do not use credentials as a substitute for argument.
- Do not use demands for civility to suppress substantive criticism.
- Do not use substantive criticism as an excuse for harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal abuse.
- Give contributors a reasonable chance to correct mistakes.
- Preserve credit and provenance.
- Report safety-sensitive concerns privately when public disclosure could create harm.

Maintainers may remove participation that consistently makes rigorous collaboration impossible.
